Shops and markets

General stores, keeper shops, the Grand Market, local prices that rise and fall, and daily notice-board orders.

General stores

Every town has one: Saltmere village, Kingsferry, Reedwick and Windle. The general store buys almost anything from your bag. Tap Sell 1 or All, and the store asks you to confirm each sale, with the price, before it goes through.

Quest and story items, pages and seals can't be sold. Riddle maps are the exception: you can sell one, but the store warns you first, because a sold map is gone for good. They show dimmed in the store ("Can't sell: you'll need this"), and no shop, market or stall will take them.

What you get depends on the town. Each town wants some things and has plenty of others.

What things are worth

Everything has a shop value, shown on its card. Things up to level 30 are cheap; past that, value climbs steeply with level (about 4 times as much at level 60, and 7 times at level 90). Something you make is never worth less than what went into it.

Local prices

  • Wants: the town pays more than the item's usual value.
  • Plenty: the town pays only 70%.
  • After a sale you hear the coins and see how many you sold and your new total.
  • An up arrow on a sell row means the town pays well for it; a down arrow means it doesn't.
  • Flooding: every item you sell drops that item's price in that town by 1%, down to half. Prices recover gradually: one sale is forgotten every 36 seconds, so a flooded price is back to normal in about half an hour.

Notice boards

Open a general store and tap Notice board. Each town keeps 3 orders on its board. Each one asks for a set amount of one item and always pays noticeably more than selling the same goods in that town (at least 1.3 times the town's own price). An order stays up for 2 in-game days (3 for big ones paying 700 coins or more), and each order shows its days left. When one is filled or runs out, a new one goes up at dawn, one slot at a time.

Tap Deliver with the full amount in your bag. You get:

  • the coins
  • Trade XP (0.1 per coin)
  • XP in the skill that made the goods (5% of the making XP)
  • a 15% chance of 1 to 3 bonus magic tiles

In Windle and Emberfall the board can also carry a drake bounty, the Joint Notice. See The Joint Notice.

The board also tells you which town Mags the Pedlar is in today.

Every market also posts one big warrant a day, with a delivery crate by the store. See Market Warrants.

Keeper shops

Some people sell things directly. Talk to them, or tap their stall, and use Buy 1 or Buy 10.

How keeper prices work:

  • They charge twice the item's value.
  • Each one you buy puts the price up 5%, up to double.
  • Stock comes back one at a time, and the price eases back down as it does.

The Grand Market

Kingsferry's Grand Market has three empty stalls. Once they're reopened (a town work), a fishmonger, a timber merchant and a smith's stall sell raw fish, logs, planks, ore and bars.

StallSells
FishmongerRaw shrimp, herring, trout, salmon
Timber merchantLogs, oak logs, willow logs, planks
Smith's stallCopper, tin and iron ore, coal, bronze and iron bars

Their prices are set so you can't buy there and resell at a profit. They're for when you'd rather pay than gather.

Reopening the market also lets you rent your own stall. See Trade and your stall.
