Riddle maps

Treasure maps that send you from place to place, solving one riddle at a time, to a chest at the end.

Getting a map

  • Buy one from Silas Quill in Kingsferry or from Mags the Pedlar, 5,000 coins each.
  • Find one. Any monster of combat level 5 or higher has a 1 in 120 chance to drop a worn riddle map.

Reading it

Tap the map and Read it. It gives you a chain of riddles, using only places you've already been. If none fit yet, hold on to it and travel more.

Each step is a single riddle. Work out where it means and:

  • Dig: stand on the right hex and tap Dig on the map's card. The map is your spade, so you don't need a tool.
  • Search: tap the right landmark from a hex next to it.

Some steps only work at dawn, dusk or night. Watch the time dial.

Rules

  • You work one map at a time. Its card shows the chain you're on.
  • Keep the map in your bag. You can't drop one by accident. You can sell it (the store warns you first), but then it and your progress are gone for good.
  • Worn maps have 3 steps.

The chest

The last step digs up a chest, and the map is used up. A worn chest holds 1,500 to 6,000 coins plus two extra rewards, such as raw sardines or Roads tiles. Anything that doesn't fit lands at your feet.

Sealed maps (4 to 5 steps) and Royal maps (6 steps) with bigger chests. Their riddles cross provinces that aren't built yet. Dyes, hats and furniture as chest rewards.
