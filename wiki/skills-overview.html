The skills

HexSeal has 15 skills. Each goes from level 1 to 99.

GroupSkills
CombatAttack, Strength, Defence, Hitpoints, Magic, Archery
GatheringWoodcutting, Fishing, Mining
MakingCooking, Crafting, Smithing, Brewing
OtherEtching (makes magic tiles), Trade (your market stall)

Every skill starts at level 1, except Hitpoints, which starts at 10. Skills feed each other: you fish, cook what you catch, eat it in a fight, and smith better tools from what you mine.

How XP works

Every action that trains a skill gives XP. Levels follow the same curve as Old School RuneScape, so each level takes a little more than the one before, and the top levels take a lot.

Total level and character level

  • Total level is all 14 levels added up. A new character has 23; the most is 1,386.
  • Character level (1 to 99) is the average of your XP across all 14 skills, read off the same curve. The free XP Hitpoints starts with doesn't count, so a new character is level 1, and only 99 in every skill makes 99.

The Skills tab

Open Skills from the dock.

  • The top strip shows your character level in a gold hex, a progress bar and your total level.
  • Each skill is a ring, four to a row. The ring fills as you head toward the next level, and the little pill shows your level.
  • Tap a ring for that skill's page:
    • your level, XP, and XP left to the next level
    • Next unlock: what your next level brings
    • Best tool: what's on your tool belt for that skill
    • Per action now: the best XP per action you can earn right now
    • The ladder: everything the skill unlocks, by level. Locked rows are dimmed and the next one is gold. Rows marked "(coming later)" aren't in the game yet.

Pinned skills

Pin up to 3 skills to the left edge of the screen: use the pin on a skill's page, or press and hold its ring. Each pinned skill shows a small ring that fills as you train, with a "+XP" as you earn it and a flash when you level. Tap one to open its page, or hold it to unpin.

When you level up, a banner slides down for three seconds with what you've unlocked, such as "You can now chop oak trees". It never blocks your taps, and several level-ups queue up.

Your character level can rise from any XP, even when no single skill levels. When it does, it gets its own banner and jingle, right after any skill banner.

The ladders already list a few tiers above what the world gives today (Frostpine trees, Emberite ore, Heartfin and up). Their items exist, but nowhere to gather them yet. See each skill's page for what you can actually gather today.

Gathering versus making

  • Gathering (Woodcutting, Fishing, Mining): tap a tree, fishing spot or rock. You keep working until your bag is full, the resource runs out, or you tap somewhere else. You need the right tool on your belt.
  • Making (Cooking, Crafting, Smithing): tap a station (a fire, a workbench or a forge) and pick what to make.

Around 1 in 150 gathering or smithing and cooking actions also turns up a magic tile. See Getting tiles.
