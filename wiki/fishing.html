How to fish

Tap a fishing spot: a ripple on the sea, a pool or a river, which you fish from the bank or shore beside it. You cast every 5 ticks (3 seconds). Fishing spots never run out.

Each catch is one of the fish that water holds, at your level and at that hour. The best fish you can catch is twice as likely as the one below it, so a higher level pays off on the same spot.

If nothing will bite, the spot says why: your level is too low for that water, or it's the wrong time of day.

Extras

  • About 1 catch in 150 brings up a Waters tile for Magic.
  • Some Magic can help here, once you've found it.
  • A full bag spills into a fish creel on your belt, if you've made one (Crafting).

Scaling boards

Each fishing region has a scaling board by the water: Moss's by the Saltmere docks, and others in Kingsferry, Mistmarsh, Windle, Thornwick, Emberfall and Frostpeak. Pick a raw fish and you keep the fish, get 1 fish scale for each one, and a little Cooking XP. Each fish can only be scaled once. Scales stack, and brewers have a use for them (see Brewing).
