The basics

Tap a monster. You walk up beside it and start swinging on your own. Melee only reaches the next hex, and never across a cliff. For fighting at range, see Magic.

There are four melee skills:

SkillWhat it does
AttackHow often you hit. Needed to wield better weapons.
StrengthHow hard you hit (your max hit).
DefenceHow often you get hit. Needed to wear better armour.
HitpointsYour health. Your max HP is your Hitpoints level. You start at 10.

Attack speed comes from your weapon: daggers and swords swing every 4 ticks (2.4 s), maces and battleaxes every 5, warhammers and greatswords every 6. Bare fists swing every 4. Tapping the monster again, or switching between targets, doesn't make you swing any sooner. Eating or a Mend holds your next swing back a little.

Each swing first rolls to hit. A miss shows a small miss. A hit always deals at least 1, up to your max hit. The same goes for monsters hitting you. Monsters fight with what they carry: archers draw and shoot, and mages cast from a staff.

Attack styles

Pick a style on the Pack tab, under your gear. It chooses which skill your XP goes to and gives a small hidden boost.

StyleTrainsBoost
Accurate (the default)AttackHit more often
AggressiveStrengthHit harder
DefensiveDefenceGet hit less

Every point of damage you deal gives 4 XP in your style's skill and 1.33 Hitpoints XP. Misses give nothing.

Tougher foes teach more. Fight something above your combat level and every hit gives extra XP, more the bigger the gap. Pick on things far below you and the XP drops off. This counts for melee, Archery and Magic alike.

Hit chance and max hit

HexSeal uses Old School RuneScape's maths.

  • Your hit chance compares your Attack level and your gear's Attack bonus against the monster's Defence.
  • Your max hit comes from your Strength level and your gear's Strength bonus.
  • Monsters roll the same way against your Defence level and your gear's Defence bonus.

Combat level

Your combat level comes from Attack, Strength, Defence, Hitpoints, Magic and Archery, using the RuneScape formula. A new character is combat level 3. Melee, Magic and Archery count as three routes; whichever is highest counts.

In a fight, each enemy shows its name and level over its health bar, coloured against your own combat level: green is easier, cream about even, orange harder and red much harder. Bosses just say Boss.

Healing

  • Eat. Tap cooked food in your bag. It's eaten on the next tick. You can't eat at full health.
  • Rest. Out of a fight, with no angry monsters within 6 hexes, you heal 1 HP a minute.
  • Fish broth heals 4 at once, then 1 a tick for 5 ticks, and has two bites. See Cooking.
  • Spells. Mend, and more you can discover. See Magic.
  • Beds and benches. Sleeping at an inn or in your The Driftwood Cottage, or resting at the Tide chapel bench (a town work) heals you fully.

Danger music

When a monster that's fighting or chasing you is within 6 hexes, the music shifts to a danger theme. It eases back once you're clear.
