Archery

Shoot bows from a few hexes off, with arrows that live in a quiver on your tool belt and mostly come back.

The short version

  • Archery is a combat skill. Wield a bow, put arrows in your quiver, and tap a monster. You walk until it's in range and in sight, then shoot on the tick.
  • Bows take both hands, so no shield.
  • Arrows go in a quiver on your tool belt, never in your bag.
  • Arrows that don't break land near the target. Walk over them and they go straight back in your quiver.
  • Archery counts toward your combat level, alongside melee and Magic. Whichever route is highest counts. See Combat level.

Getting started

Captain Hale Yarrow runs the archery butts in Windle (Highmoor). He only teaches people who make their own arrows, and his quest, First Feather, shows you how. Old Tamsin at the Salted Eel will point you his way.

Before that, the straw targets at the butts give 8 Archery XP a hit, up to Archery 10.

The butts shop sells feathers, bronze, iron and steel arrows, flax bowstrings, a canvas quiver and a wooden shortbow, so you can start shooting before you can make everything yourself.

  • Arrows fly over water and down off terraces and cliffs. Shooting down from higher ground reaches 1 hex further.
  • You can't shoot through trees or walls, or up onto a terrace higher than you.
  • Your hit chance comes from your Archery level and your gear's Archery bonus. Bows, vambraces and archer's armour all add to it.
  • Your max hit comes from your Archery level and your arrow's strength, plus a longbow's draw.
  • A bow only takes arrows up to its own tier. If the arrows at the front of your quiver are too good for the bow in hand, it swaps in the best kind it can take.

Each point of damage gives 4 XP (Longrange splits it, 2 Archery and 2 Defence) and 1.33 Hitpoints XP.

Armoured and light foes

Arrows do a quarter less against armoured foes and a quarter more against light ones. The monster card shows which a monster is. Golems, sentries and knights tend to be armoured; wolfmen, bandits and drakes are light. See Monsters.

Your quiver

The quiver sits on your tool belt and holds arrows with no stack limit. Without a proper quiver, about 4 in 5 arrows come back.

Archer's armour

Light leather that needs Defence and Archery at its level, and adds to your Archery hit chance as well as your Defence. Each set has a coif, body, chaps and vambraces. Vambraces give the most Archery bonus.

See Archer's armour for each piece.

Where to buy

  • The Windle butts (Highmoor): feathers, arrows, flax bowstrings, a canvas quiver and a wooden shortbow.
  • Ossie Nock's bench in Thornwick (Greenwood): feathers, arrow shafts, and unstrung oak, willow and ash bows.
  • The Old Mill (Fairmeadow): leather and tallow.
