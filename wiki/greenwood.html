Greenwood

Old forest around the foresters' town of Thornwick, with elderwood, salmon and lantern-owls.

At a glance

An old, deep forest around Thornwick, a foresters' town. Good for levels 20 to 45. "The trees close in. Travellers 20+ advised."

Places: Thornwick, the Elder Tree, the Hollow and the North-West Gate.

Getting here: take the Kingsway north out of Mistmarsh.

What's here

SkillWhere to train
WoodcuttingElderwood trees (level 60) for elder logs, plus ash (30), willows and oaks
FishingThe river and a lily pond. Ask Old Wade at the weir.
MiningHexslate
Crafting, CookingWorkbench and campfire
EtchingThe Warden of Roots statue, for Roots tiles (Etching 25)
BrewingElderflower, greyleaf in the Hollow, and the root kettle

There's no forge here.

Services: a bank in a green tower, a general store, the Lost Lantern inn, Dunstan's lumbermill, and a Roadwarden post box (deposit only: whatever you drop in turns up in your bank).

At night the general store and the mill shut. Everyone's at the Lost Lantern.

Waystone: Thornwick, and another near the North-West Gate.

People

Hesper Vane (head forester), Granny Burl (reads the bark of the Elder Tree), Kip Lantern (keeps the lantern-owls), Dunstan Oakes (runs the mill) Abel Thorne (one of Dunstan's woodcutters), and Ossie Nock, a bowyer whose bench sells bows and fletching supplies. See Archery.

Hesper gives this region's map quest, The Forester's Count. See Maps.

Danger

This is a step up from the south. Outlaws work the Kingsway by day, the Bone Guard walk the deep wood, and at night something else comes out. See Monsters.

Mending house: the Lost Lantern. Fee 120. Kip's lantern-owls find whoever falls in the wood.

Guard's Rests: two, Rootwatch and Greywatch. See Bones and the Guard's Rest.

Roads out

WayLeads to
Kingsway south to MistmarshMistmarsh
The Cold Road east to FrostpeakFrostpeak. "Travellers 35+ advised."
The Crown Valley's North-West Gate (sealed)The Crown Valley, once it opens. It's past the Hollow.

The signpost by the Hollow says "The trees there are dead. Turn back." It means it. Its cold wears you down; a Thawwater keeps it out. See Harsh places.
