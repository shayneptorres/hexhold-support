      Skills

      Every skill, how to train it, and what it unlocks.

      • Skills and levels All 15 skills, how XP and levels work, your character level, and how to read the Skills tab.
      • Tools and the tool belt Axes, pickaxes, rods, hammers and chisels hang on your belt, out of your bag. Better tools work faster.
      • Woodcutting Chop trees for logs. Logs feed Crafting (staffs, rods, sacks, chests) and twine.
      • Fishing Catch fish with a rod. Cook them to eat in fights, or sell them.
      • Mining Mine ore for Smithing, and hexslate for Etching. Which rocks there are, and where to find them.
      • Smithing Smelt ore into bars at a forge, then hammer bars into weapons, armour and tools.
      • Cooking Cook fish on a fire, and bake bread, pies and cakes in an oven, so you can eat them to heal. Higher levels burn less.
      • Crafting Turn logs into staffs, rods, shields, bows and arrows, make archer's armour and pottery, plus twine, planks, gathering sacks, field chests and bundles.
      • Magic Cast spells by laying hex tiles in the rosette. No runes, no fixed order, and a failed try never costs you a tile.
      • Archery Partly in gameShoot bows from a few hexes off, with arrows that live in a quiver on your tool belt and mostly come back.
      • Etching Partly in gameMake your own magic tiles. Mine hexslate, smelt it into wardglass, and etch it at a Warden statue.
      • Trade and your stall Rent a stall in Kingsferry's Grand Market, set your prices, and let shoppers buy while you're off doing other things.
      • Brewing Partly in gameForage wild herbs, brew draughts at a still, and drink them from 4-sip bottles to be better at things for a while.
      • Muscle Memory Partly in gameOnce you know a skill well enough, your character keeps working at it while the game is closed, slower than playing.

      Economy

      Coins, banks, shops, markets and trade.

      • Bag, bank and coins Your 28-slot bag, the shared bank with 200 slots, and where coins come from and go.
      • Shops and markets General stores, keeper shops, the Grand Market, local prices that rise and fall, and daily notice-board orders.
      • Sacks, chests and bundles Partly in gameCarry more than 28 things. Belt sacks catch overflow, field chests hold your haul on site, bundles pack many items into one slot, and the King's Post and its rooks carry loads to the bank.
      • The Driftwood Cottage Your home on Saltmere. Fix it up room by room for a bed, a hearth and a larder.
      • Town works Partly in gamePay to fix up the towns. Each repair changes the town for good and opens a lasting perk.
      • Mags the Pedlar A wandering pedlar who sets up in a different town each day, with four rare things for sale.
      • Riddle maps Partly in gameTreasure maps that send you from place to place, solving one riddle at a time, to a chest at the end.
      • Market Warrants One big order a day at every market. Fill it for about two and a half times shop value, Trade XP and a Market Seal.

      World

      Places, travel, maps and quests.

      • The Wardlands The world of HexSeal as the islanders tell it. Just enough to get your bearings; the rest is yours to uncover.
      • Saltmere Isle The windy island where you wake up. A fishing village, a quay, woods, a quarry, and an old fort on the headland.
      • Fairmeadow Farmland on the river Brin and the trading port of Kingsferry, home of the Grand Market.
      • Mistmarsh A wide, foggy marsh of reed beds, bog and black meres around the stilt village of Reedwick.
      • Highmoor Wide, windy moorland, fells, tarns and windmills around the hill town of Windle.
      • Getting around Walking between regions, the ferry, waystones, and teleport spells.
      • Greenwood A big old forest around the foresters' town of Thornwick: the Deepwood, Wendmere, the Highwood ridge, the Alder fen and the grey wood by the gate.
      • Cinderreach Red badlands under the smoking Kiln, around the smithing town of Emberfall.
      • Maps Each region's map has to be earned. The world map stays under cloud until you do.
      • Frostpeak Snowbound mountains around the Delvers' town of Kettlepass and its hot spring.
      • Quests Partly in gameHow quests start and how to keep track, plus every quest in the game so far. No solutions here.
      • The Crown Valley Partly in gameThe grey valley inside the Crownwall, ringed by six gates, with the Hexhold at its heart.
      • Wyrmholm A smoking island off Cinderreach where drakes nest. Late-game, reached only by Cass's skiff.
