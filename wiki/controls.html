Touch controls

GestureWhat it does
Tap a hexWalk there, one hex per tick
Talk to someoneTheir words open on a story card with their portrait, name and role. Long speeches come a few lines at a time: tap Next. Any choices are on the last page. Signs and stones use a plain sheet.
Tap a tree, rock, person or monsterWalk next to it and use it: chop, mine, fish, talk, attack, bank. Tapping anywhere on their hex counts, except for a calm Bone Guard: tap the Guard itself to fight it, so you never start one by accident.
Tap a pile of items, or your own hexPick up what's there
Tap a signpostA card with where each arm points
Tap a building you can enterGo into… steps inside, unless something you're fighting, or chasing you close behind, is on you. Stations by the door still work from outside.
Long-press anythingA "What is this?" card with its name, what it needs, and buttons for what you can do
Drag with one fingerTurn the camera around you
PinchZoom in and out
The zoom slider above the menuZoom with one thumb. Drag the bar, or tap + or −. The top is closest. It sits on the right, or the left in the left-handed layout, and Settings can hide it.
Pinch in past the closest zoomAdmire your hero up close. Pinch out or walk to go back.
Tap the place name or compassOpen the map
Long-press the compassTurn the camera to face north

Panels like Magic and the Journal, and the long-press card, close with the × in their corner, or by tapping their tab again. Tapping the world while a menu is open also closes it, and that tap does nothing else, so you won't walk off by accident.

Tapping open floor where a villager stands walks you to the nearest free hex beside them. You can still walk onto their hex to pick up something dropped there.

Long-press is how you look closely at things. When a quest needs you to look at something, a normal tap on it works too.

The top of the screen

  • Place name: the area you're in. It changes as you walk. Tap it for the map.
  • Tick dot: flashes on every game tick.
  • Health: a heart with your hitpoints, top right. It cracks when you drop below a third.
  • Compass: a brass compass whose rose turns with the camera, with a red N. Six gems round the rim stand for the six provinces; each lights up once you have its map. After a fall, it also points to your grave.
  • Time dial: the sun and moon cross its sky as the day goes by. See Day and night.
  • Pinned skills: up to 3 skill rings down the left edge. See Pinned skills.
  • Boost badge: under the place name while a draught is working. Tap it for the time left. See Brewing.
  • Hands chip: under the place name. Lit when leaving the app would let your character keep working. See Muscle Memory.

Up to 4 HUD pins sit on the right, above the dock: bottles, food or other things from your bag you want one tap away. Hold an item in your bag and tap Pin to HUD.

Messages, XP drops and item notes appear briefly in the middle. You can turn XP and item popups off in Settings; important messages always show.

The dock

The bar at the bottom has five tabs:

TabWhat's in it
PackYour worn gear, tool belt and 28-slot bag
SkillsLevels, XP and what each skill unlocks
MagicThe tile satchel, rosette and spellbook
JournalYour quests, rumours and maps. See Quests.
···Map and Settings

Tap an open tab again to close it. Left-handed layout in Settings mirrors the dock.

A quest you've pinned in the Journal shows its next step in a strip above the dock, bottom right.

Playing on a foldable

On an iPhone Duo, the game follows the hinge:

  • Closed, or fully open and upright: one screen, like any iPhone.
  • Half open and upright: the menus stay open on one half and the game plays on the other. The game sits above the menus, or below them with game below the menu in Settings.
  • Open sideways: one wide landscape screen. Menus open in a phone-width column at the right (the left, with the left-handed layout), and you can keep tapping the world beside them. Turn on side by side in Settings to give the game and the menus a half each instead. The zoom slider and your pins sit at the far edge of the screen, just above the menu.

In a split, tapping the game's half walks as normal and leaves the menu open.
