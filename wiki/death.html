Death and the mending house

Fall in a fight and you wake at the nearest mending house. Your things wait in a grave that never expires.

What happens when you fall

  1. "Oh dear, you are dead!" The screen fades, and you wake at the mending house of the region you fell in, at full health. If the last bed you slept in, at an inn or at home, is in that region, you wake in that bed instead, for free. See Beds.
  2. The mender charges a fee.
  3. Everything you were carrying waits for you in a grave near where you fell.

The fall is saved straight away, so closing the app doesn't undo it.

The fee

The fee comes from your bag's coins first, then your bank. If you can't cover it, the rest goes on your tab, and half of every coin you earn goes toward it until it's paid off.

Take a room at the inn before a risky trip. Fall in that province and you wake in the bed you paid for, with no fee. Your The Driftwood Cottage bed works the same way on Saltmere.

Your grave

  • Your bag, your worn gear and what's in your sacks go into a grave on the nearest clear hex. If you fall indoors, it's by that building's door.
  • It never expires. A small headstone on the compass points the way.
  • Tap the grave to take everything back. Gear goes back on first. It's free.
  • You always keep your tool belt, the sacks themselves, and quest items.

Falling again before you get back

If you fall a second time before reaching your grave, the old grave crumbles and its contents go to that region's mender, in the lost-and-found. You can buy them back for 10 times the region's fee, or the value of the things if that's less.

The lost-and-found keeps one lot. Fall a third time before buying it back and that older lot is gone for good.

When nothing is lost

Falling on Saltmere before you've finished your first days there costs nothing and leaves no grave: "Someone found you and kept your things safe."

Hard mode

Tick Hard mode on the title screen before starting a new game. In Hard mode, falling destroys your bag and worn gear, with no grave. Fees are the same. You still keep your tool belt, what's in your sacks, and quest items.

A one-life mode.
