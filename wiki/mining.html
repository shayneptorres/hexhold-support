How to mine

Tap a rock. You swing every 4 ticks (2.4 seconds). When a rock runs out it goes dull and comes back after a short wait.

Hexslate is the stone Etching starts from. It never runs out. Greyed slate etches into Hollow tiles, and sealstone and hexstone smelt into the two highest bars so far. See Smithing.

Spoiler Reveals: ores deeper in
  • Starstone is in the Delvings' first floor, in the Shade halls and the Starstone cut.
  • Duskite is on the Delvings' second floor, in the Duskite cut.
  • Sealstone, greyed slate and hexstone are in the Crown Valley, and wyrmglass is on Wyrmholm.

Stones that are only scenery, like carved walls, standing stones, rails and rubble, can't be mined.

Extras

  • About 1 ore in 150 comes with a Stone tile for Magic.
  • Some Magic can help here, once you've found it.
  • A full bag spills into an ore sack on your belt, if you've made one (Crafting).

Emberite and rimesilver smelt into the bars between steel and the top tiers; see Smithing.
