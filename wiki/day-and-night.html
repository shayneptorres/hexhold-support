Day and night

A full day lasts 20 minutes. Ferries stop, shops close and different things come out after dark.

The tick

Everything in HexSeal happens on a game tick of 0.6 seconds, like Old School RuneScape. You walk one hex per tick, and gathering, fighting and respawning all happen on ticks.

A day

A full day is 2,000 ticks, or 20 minutes of play. The clock only runs while you're playing. It stops when the app is closed and while the bank is open. New games start in the morning.

The time dial at the top shows a sun by day, a moon at night and orange at dusk and dawn, and its ring fills through the day.

What changes at night

  • The ferry doesn't sail.
  • Some shops close, including Ida's stall on Saltmere and the general store and curio shop in Kingsferry.
  • People keep their own hours. Some only work by day, others only come out at night. Anyone a quest is waiting on stays out after dark so you can still find them.
  • Some monsters only come out at night, others only by day. See Monsters.
  • Windows glow, lamp posts light the streets, and lighthouses light up. Lamps and lit windows throw warm light on the ground near you.
  • Some riddle steps only work at dawn, dusk or night.

Beds

Every inn lets rooms from dusk to dawn. After dark, tapping the inn from outside offers Sleep through the night under the way in: one tap pays, sleeps and leaves you outside in the morning. Inside, just tap the stairs or bed to pay and sleep. By day a tap tells you rooms open at dusk. You can also long-press it, or ask the innkeeper: "A room for the night?" Pay, sleep through to morning, and wake at full health.

The last bed you slept in, an inn's or your own, is where you wake if you fall in that province, with no fee. Fall anywhere else and you wake at that region's mender as usual. See Death and the mending house.
