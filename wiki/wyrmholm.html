Wyrmholm

A smoking island off Cinderreach where drakes nest. Late-game, reached only by Cass's skiff.

At a glance

A black-sand island of ash, glass and steam off the Cinderreach coast. Drakes nest in its middle, and the ground is warm wherever you stand. It's for seasoned fighters: combat level 60 at the very least, and more is wise.

Places: the black-sand shore, the ash ground, the obsidian ridge, the crater lake, the lava pools, the drake nests and the Wyrm's Cairn.

Getting there

Cass keeps a little skiff in the shallows off Emberfall. When it's seaworthy, Cass will row you out to "the smoking rock", by day only. "Not at night. Things fly at night."

The way there opens through two hidden Cinderreach quests, late in the game. Listen for rumours of smoke in Windle. See Hidden quests.

Spoiler Reveals: the quests' names, and what opens the first

They're Smoke at Night and The Anvil Song. Smoke at Night (from Bryn Hollis in Windle) asks for combat level 60, and for the Flame quest line to be finished, up to Never Let the Anvil Cool.

There's no bank, shop or waystone on the island.

Danger

The heat. Out by the nests and the crater, heat comes up in waves and takes 2 health every 12 ticks. A Cinderward draught keeps it off. See Brewing.

Drakes nest in the middle of the island: rust drakes near the shore, ember drakes deeper in, and darker ones up on the ridge. They don't like visitors. See Monsters.

Drake breath

Every so often a drake takes a deep breath, and the hexes in front of it toward you glow orange. A couple of ticks later they burn. Step off the glow and you take nothing.

DrakeBreathBurns for
Rust drakeA short line, 2 hexes8 to 14
Ember drakeA cone, 3 hexes deep12 to 20
Obsidian drakeA cone, 3 hexes deep, more often16 to 26

If it does catch you, a Cinderward halves the burn, a drakehide or Wyrmscale body halves it again, and a Wyrmscale shield takes another quarter off. Drake-made bodies also keep off the island's heat. See Drake gear.

The Joint Notice

Once Cass's skiff is running, the notice boards in Windle and Emberfall post a drake bounty each dawn: kill 6 to 12 drakes of any kind, then hand it in at either board. It pays coins per drake (more for the bigger ones), a few drake scales, and now and then a riddle map or a curio.

Mending house: fall here and you wake in Emberfall, at Old Bellows' burn shed. Fee 250.

The Wyrm's Cairn

A cairn of stacked black glass, taller than a man, with enormous bones laid at its foot. Every drake drops dragon bones, and laying them at the cairn seems to please the brood. See Bones and the Guard's Rest.

Spoiler Reveals: what the cairn does
  • Each set of bones laid gives you Cairn's warmth: the island's heat can't touch you for 3 minutes.
  • The brood takes notice at 10 and 25. At 50, rust drakes stop starting fights with you, though they'll still fight back.

The end of the line

The drake quests end with a third, from Old Bellows. It's the hardest fight in Cinderreach. Bring good food, a Cinderward, and a Water spell or two.

Spoiler Reveals: what waits at the end, and how the fight works (not the puzzles)
  • Under the Kiln sleeps Old Cinder, the mother of the brood (level 85, 640 health, hits up to 9). Tamsy blows you a seal-glass from 5 wyrmglass, and you'll need a Cinderward or the Cairn's warmth to go in.
  • On the ground she breathes in a cone, sweeps her tail and bites. Step off the glowing hexes.
  • At 60% health she takes to the smoke. Her fireballs leave the floor burning, and a Water spell puts it out. The four anvil-bells round the cave bring her down, stunned for 20 ticks, if you ring them in the right order. (Bellows taught you the order.)
  • Under 25% she's enraged, and the floor cracks open with lava.
  • At 0 she yields, and you put her back to sleep. Ysolde and Bellows will want to hear about it. Your first fight with her is a little gentler than later ones.
  • Afterwards: the Anvil Council's Quiet the Kiln bounty pays 25,000 coins and a trophy. Ring the bells out of order and she wakes again for a rematch, which pays the Kept the Kiln Quiet bounty (4,000 coins) after a rest between fights.
  • Cass gives you a wyrmling: a pet that follows you, curls up by warm things, and puffs a smoke ring when you cook. Lose it, and Cass will find you another.

Lore

Emberfall's smiths say the smoke used to come only from the Kiln. Old Bellows says his master sang to the Kiln every night, and won't say why. Cass says the island has always been there, and that's all Cass will say. Lately, Emberfall folk say the Kiln breathes easier at night.
