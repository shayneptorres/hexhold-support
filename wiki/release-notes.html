About these notes

HexSeal isn't released yet. These are notes for the test builds on the way there. The build number is the one shown at the bottom of Settings. When the game launches, these notes will be archived and the release notes will start fresh.

Notes cover what changed for players, never story beats or secrets.

November 22, 2026

Build 989 (TestFlight 989)

  • Emberite and rimesilver can be mined. Emberite (Mining 30) is in Cinderreach and rimesilver (Mining 40) in Frostpeak, so embersteel and frostsilver gear can be made. There's more coal in Highmoor and Greenwood, more iron in Greenwood, and more of the deeper ores where they belong. See Where to mine.
  • Scenery stones no longer mine like ore.
  • Tougher foes teach more. Fighting above your level gives extra combat XP, and picking on much weaker foes gives less.
  • People of level 12 and up carry more coins and sometimes a cooked fish.
  • Doors and beds only turn you away while something is actually on you. Monsters that can't catch you give up and go home.
  • Tap your grave to take everything at once, or pick things out one at a time. Long-press still has Take all. See Your grave.
  • The ruin on Spire Hill is solid now, so you walk round its walls instead of through them.
  • Mags the Pedlar has her own hooded look, and a face on her story card. Every speaker now shows a face.
  • Old Corra greets you by your title again.
  • In Mistmarsh, a quest's reed-cutting spot is easier to find: the reeds stand tall where they grow, and a plain tap cuts them.

November 20, 2026

Build 988 (TestFlight 988)

  • Greenwood, Cinderreach and Frostpeak are full size, as big as Fairmeadow, Mistmarsh and Highmoor. Each has new areas to explore, new signposts, places to look out from, and five new kinds of monster. See Greenwood, Cinderreach, Frostpeak and Monsters.
  • Greenwood gains the Deepwood, Wendmere, the Highwood ridge, the Charwood, the Greying and the Alder fen, with still-water and sea fishing and more elderwood.
  • Cinderreach gains the Scorch mesa, the Glass Sands, the Steamwells and the old workings, with much more ore and firethorn.
  • Frostpeak climbs in steps up to five levels high: the Switchbacks to the Delvings, the Fells, the Rimewood, the Barrows, and the Shoulder with Hornhold on top. More ore, rimecap and ice holes.
  • Saves made before this build carry over: wherever you were in these three regions, you pick up in the same spot on the bigger map.
  • The treasure charts for Greenwood, Cinderreach and Frostpeak are redrawn for the new maps.
  • A zoom slider above the menu zooms with one thumb. It replaces the tap-and-hold zoom, which kept going off by accident. Settings can hide it. On an open iPhone Duo held sideways, the slider and your pins sit at the far edge above the menu.
  • At night, tap an inn from outside to sleep through the night without going in.

November 19, 2026

Builds 943 to 987 (TestFlight 987)

Build numbers now count TestFlight releases, so they jump here.

  • Foldables. HexSeal now plays well on the iPhone Duo, with split, wide and side-by-side layouts. See Playing on a foldable.
  • Tapping the world while a menu is open closes the menu, and nothing else happens.
  • The bank's Sort groups by kind, and a second tap sorts A to Z.
  • The "how many?" menu and item cards open beside the slot you held.
  • Tapping food eats the one you tapped, and banking moves the slot you tapped.
  • After a sale you see how many sold and your new coin total.
  • A Carter's crate in the Saltmere, Kingsferry and Windle forges sends your smithing to the bank.
  • The Pack beside a shop or station shares the screen instead of pushing it away.
  • Settings sliders work with a sideways swipe, and an up or down swipe scrolls.
  • Tapping a villager's spot walks you beside them instead of on top of them.
  • A tricky step in a Saltmere quest has clearer signs now.
  • A region with no map yet tells you how to earn it.
  • Story cards always show a face or a picture, and fill in every name.
  • A new start-up screen with the HexSeal logo.

October 21, 2026

Build 942

  • The character preview in your Pack mirrors you more closely: it shows your rod and line while you fish, and takes on your disguise while a disguise draught lasts.

October 20, 2026

Build 929

  • The Magic tab is redesigned: taller, with your tile satchel at the top.
  • The rosette folds away while you browse and names the spell as you lay tiles.
  • Element filters can be combined, and you can pin favourite spells to the top.

Build 921

  • Sheep and cows graze in pens by Fairmeadow's farmsteads. They never fight back, and give raw meat, fleece and cowhide.
  • Cowhide tans into leather at a workbench.
  • Smoother walking on 120 Hz screens.
  • Spells and arrows follow a moving target.

Build 903

  • Pottery arrives as part of Crafting. Dig clay at riverbanks in four regions, shape it at a potter's wheel and fire it in a kiln. Firing can crack a piece, less often as your Crafting improves.
  • Prue Crock, a potter by the Brin in Fairmeadow, teaches the craft.
  • A pie dish helps your pies bake, and a brew crock holds more sips than a bottle.

Build 890

  • New wells beside bread ovens and mills, and water butts by the hearth in two taverns.
  • A new spell to discover, for carrying water.

Build 858

Build 838

  • Farmstead and farmhouse ovens now only bake; tavern hearths still work as fires.

  • Fleece has a proper look.

Build 818

Build 813

Build 782

  • New moorland creatures roam.

Build 767

  • Highmoor has wind: gusts in the sky, drifting cloud shadows, and windmill sails that turn everywhere, faster in a Highmoor gust.

Build 763

Build 739

Build 647

  • When a quest asks you to look at something, a normal tap does it now, not just the press-and-hold card.

Build 548

  • Clearer quest talk: quest conversations read better, replies fit what was said, and some pages give you two to pick from. Turning down a quest no longer gets you asked again on every visit.

Build 508

Build 499

  • Longer-lasting orders: Market Warrants now stay up for 3 real days, and a new one goes up the day after you fill one. Part-done warrants still pay for what you delivered.

Build 471

Build 463

Build 458

Build 450

  • Brews are now bottles. Each brew makes a 4-sip bottle in your pack; tap it to drink. Bottles stack in the bank. The flask belt and the bank's Cellar tab are gone, and any sips you had are waiting in your bank as bottles.

  • Cooking fish no longer gives fish scales.

Build 447

  • A Brews tab in the Magic menu, beside Spells, with your flasks, your cellar and a brewing book of herbs, where they grow and where to brew. Belt flasks show their names, and helpful tips suggest a brew when you need one.
  • Enemy name tags: in a fight, enemies show their name and level, coloured by how tough they are for you.
  • Fairmeadow's foothills quarry has more copper and tin, and now iron and coal too.

Build 442

  • New music: a warm village waltz for Thornwick, a low, uneasy theme for Wyrmholm's shore, and danger music for the island's deepest places.

Build 436

  • The drake quests reach their end with a third quest from Old Bellows, and a fight Cinderreach will talk about for years. Two new bounties come with it, and someone small and warm wants to follow you home.

Build 432

  • Drakes breathe fire now. Watch for the orange glow and step out of it.
  • Drake gear: drakehide and Wyrmscale armour, a wyrmglass staff, and a wyrmfang spear, axe and pickaxe, each with a trick of its own.
  • The Joint Notice, a daily drake bounty on the Windle and Emberfall notice boards.
  • Kilnfin and sealsteel rivets have their own looks.

Build 429

  • The outdoor forges on Saltmere, in Kingsferry and in Windle are now proper smithies, with a roof, a smoking chimney and a hearth that glows at night. Stand in front of the hearth to work.

Build 426

  • Every general store, and Kingsferry's curio shop and stables, are now bigger buildings.
  • A few things moved a step: the warrant crates on Saltmere and in Highmoor, the Reedwick store's door, and a rimecap patch in Frostpeak.

Build 422

  • Lamp posts light the town streets at night, and lit windows and lamps throw warm light on the ground around you.
  • Cottages, farmsteads, shops, stables, wells, crates and hay bales are now sized to match each other.

Build 419

  • Fairmeadow is reworked again: four bridges over the Brin, farmsteads in the fields, gentle terraces from the harbour up to the gate, and a cobbled market square in Kingsferry.
  • You can now fish the Brin itself, from the bank. Greenwood's river spots work the same way.

Build 415

  • Kingsferry's cottages, watermill, warehouses, stables and boatyard now stand at their proper size.
  • One of the late town works now leaves something growing by your cottage.

October 16, 2026

Build 409

  • After dark, a single tap on an inn's stairs or bed takes the room and puts you to sleep.

Build 407

  • Something is stirring in Cinderreach. Two new late-game quests start from Windle and Emberfall, for fighters of combat level 60 and up.
  • Wyrmholm, a smoking island off Cinderreach, with drakes, emberleaf trees, wyrmglass rocks and a rare lava fish. Bring a Cinderward.
  • A new spell, Kindle, and sealsteel rivets at the forge.

Build 404

  • Rooms at every inn. Inns let rooms from dusk to dawn, at the bed or from the innkeeper, and four inns have new keepers. The last bed you slept in is where you wake if you fall in that province, for free.
  • One ore bin for every forge. Ore left at one village forge is waiting at the others. Your old bins are merged, and nothing is lost.
  • New late-game town works for players who've come a long way.

Build 392

  • A much bigger Fairmeadow. The province is redrawn with hills and terraces, a larger Kingsferry and Spire Hill. Its map chart is redrawn to match.
  • If your save was in Fairmeadow, you'll wake somewhere safe nearby, and any field chests you'd left there are waiting in your bank.
  • Fishing spots sit right on the shore now, and the Fairmeadow post box is back, on the Spire Hill road.
  • The keep at Gullwatch Point has a door now.
  • The title screen's fly-bys show the new Fairmeadow.

Build 384

  • Brewing polish. Some draughts now meet the world in new ways, and there's a new puzzle for brewers who like a disguise.
  • Masters of Brewing get a livelier mantle, a new way to be greeted, and a moment of their own.

Build 380

  • Mistmarsh and Highmoor change at night. Different things walk the roads after dark, shops close, windows glow, and Reedwick has a bell to listen for at midnight.
  • The While you were away card now lists the draught sips you used.
  • The day clock keeps running while you're on the bank's Cellar tab.

Build 376

  • Every quest conversation has been rewritten. Quest givers now ask before a quest starts (say no and they'll ask again), tell you where things stand once you've started, and have a last word when you're done. Your own replies have more character.
  • Anyone a quest is waiting on stays out after dark so you can still find them. Shops still close at night.

Build 370

  • Two more town works: restore the guild hall in Kingsferry for a fourth notice-board order each day, and re-roof the foresters' lodge in Thornwick, where logs then sell for 30% more.
  • Fine pieces from the quench trough earn a little extra Trade XP.

Build 361

  • Higher-level things are worth more. Items past level 30 now sell for much more the higher their level, and anything you make is worth at least what went into it.
  • Early warrants pay a little less; later ones are unchanged.
  • Six big new town works: an eel smokery in Reedwick, a shearing barn in Windle, a bowyer's loft in Thornwick, the Emberfall bell and a gilded Anvil Council in Emberfall, and a fur trader in Kettlepass.

Build 356

  • Market Warrants. Every market now posts one big order a day, matched to your skills, at a delivery crate by the general store. It pays well, gives Trade XP and a Market Seal, and renews at midnight.
  • Fill any five in a week for the Market Clerks' chest.
  • Spend Market Seals on new things for your cottage.
  • Runners and rooks can deliver straight to a warrant's crate.

Build 351

  • The village forges. The forges on Saltmere, in Kingsferry and in Windle now have an ore bin your runners and rooks can fill, a strongbox that opens your bank, bellows that speed up every job, and a quench trough that sometimes turns out a Fine piece.
  • Two new smiths: Aggie Brand in Kingsferry and Morwen Hollis in Windle. Brannoc has tips.
  • The forge's list now has a tab for each metal.

Build 346

  • The Crown Valley. The Crownwall's gates can open at last, onto the grey valley around the Hexhold. It's the toughest place yet, for levels 65 to 90.
  • The Lantern camp inside the South Gate has a bank chest, a campfire, a waystone and a mender.
  • New trees, rocks and fish: duskwood, hexwood, sealstone, greyed slate, hexstone and hexcarp, with sealsteel and hexsteel gear to smith. The valley has its own music.
  • New monsters, and someone on the bridge who won't let you pass.
  • The Captain's Rest, a set of duels for players with total level 750, and a new map quest from Old Hob.
  • Crownwall Gate (Magic 68) teleports you inside any gate you've walked through.
  • Fixed where you wake up after being knocked out in Fairmeadow.

Build 341

  • Two more long quest lines, from Hesper Vane in Thornwick and Tamsy Glass in Emberfall. Like the last three, each is a puzzle across its province, and each ends with something that changes your Magic.
  • Fixed: a quest item handed to you with a full bag now goes to your bank instead of being lost.

Build 336

  • A friendlier Saltmere. The island's mentors remember you and how far you got, your answers have more character, and Hattie tells you which jobs are left.
  • Brannoc keeps his forge open at any hour while you're learning.
  • Your first drowned oarsman always drops a Waters tile, and Tamsin's magic lesson is clearer.
  • Something new above the Salted Eel's hearth.

Build 333

  • Three new quest lines, from Old Hob in Kingsferry, Tobin Reed in Reedwick and Old Corra at the Sixstones. Each is a puzzle that crosses its province, and the end of each one changes your Magic.

Build 330

  • Post rooks. Ida on Saltmere has a new quest, Ida's Rumour Mill. Finish it and a rook will fly one stack to your bank for a fish, from almost anywhere outdoors.
  • A spell you may know can now send a whole field chest home.
  • New sounds for the King's Post and the rooks.
  • Fixed a rare freeze when starting a conversation.

Build 327

  • The King's Post. A new quest in Kingsferry, The Last Toll, puts a post horn on your belt. Blow it and a runner carries your resources to the bank for a small fee.
  • Ruined post houses wait in the other provinces, and Roadwarden post boxes take deposits straight to your bank.
  • New things to make: iron nails at the forge and signboards at the workbench.
  • Small fixes to the story card.

Build 323

  • Every conversation now uses the story card, with the speaker's portrait, name and role. Long speeches come a few lines at a time, and passing remarks, rumours and mentor tips wait for your tap.
  • Anything said while another screen is open waits its turn.

Build 320

  • The Delvings now sound like a mine: dripping caves, torches, chains, the lift and the carts, and more.

Build 318

  • The Delvings, all the way down. The rest of the mine is open, to the very bottom. Bring plenty of food.
  • A new big fish bites off Saltmere at night, for very experienced anglers.

Build 314

  • The Delvings. Gunna Delve has work for you at the mine gate in Frostpeak. It's the start of a new quest line, with the first part of the mine to explore. Bring a light.

Build 309

  • Pin your skills. Keep up to 3 skills on the left of the screen and watch them fill as you train.
  • Monsters now carry their weapons. Archers shoot and mages cast from staffs, and every monster has a small chance to drop a weapon.
  • You put your weapon away to cast, each kind of spell has its own move, and your character eats with their hands free.
  • Misses now show a small "miss".

October 15, 2026

Build 295

  • A spot tells you when your level is too low or it's the wrong hour.
  • Pike can be caught now.
  • Fixed: some indoor furniture could be fished or gathered from.

Build 290

  • You now kneel at a Guard's Rest to lay bones down.
  • In combat, a hit that lands always does at least 1 damage, and misses show a "miss" puff instead of a 0.

Build 285

  • Fish broth now takes only small fish, from shrimp up to trout.
  • Fixed: inspecting a spot near the mine cart could send you on a cart ride the next time you walked.

Build 281

  • Better fish are worth more. Each step up the fishing ladder now heals more and sells for more, and item cards show how much food heals. See Healing.

Build 277

  • More sound. Brewing, every spell, teleports, doors, coins, the Journal and more now have their own sounds, and a chime plays the first time you reach a new place. Turn them up or down under Sound effects in Settings.

Build 272

  • Story cards. Quest starts, new steps and finished quests now open a big card with the speaker's portrait. The world waits while you read.
  • Portraits in the Journal and on the pinned quest line.
  • Brewing is easier to follow: you're told how many sips went to your cellar, empty flasks fill themselves, and Hattie explains the cellar after your first brew.
  • Your weapon is put away while you work, and brewing, crafting and digging have new animations.
  • Refreshed rooms, and the northern villagers have their own looks.

Build 266

  • Shape draughts. Three new draughts change what you are for a short while. Tap the badge to end one early.
  • Brewing 99 now has its reward, and it's a big one.
  • The Old Mill in Fairmeadow sells flour.

Build 262

  • Brewing comes north. Five new herbs (Brewing 20 to 60), a brewing station in every northern province, and eleven new draughts, including a few wards.
  • The Hollow, the Kiln and Frostpeak's night snow now wear you down while you're out in them. The right draught keeps them off.
  • Brewing perks at 30, 40, 65 and 70.
  • Magic tiles dropped by monsters now lie flat in the pile, and a message says when they go to your tile satchel.

Build 257

  • Frostpeak is open, the last of the six provinces. Take the Cold Road from Greenwood or Cinderreach, or ride the mine cart from Emberfall by day, to the snowbound Delvers' town of Kettlepass and its hot spring.
  • Frostfin to catch through the ice (Fishing 40), a Stone statue for Etching, its own map quest and Guard's Rest, and the toughest monsters yet, for levels 35 to 65.
  • Cinderreach's ground is now a sandy gold.

Build 252

  • Cinderreach is open. Take the Kingsway north from Highmoor to the red badlands under the Kiln and the smithing town of Emberfall, with its great furnace, its own map quest, a Guard's Rest and a Flame statue for Etching.
  • Gold to mine and smelt (Mining and Smithing 45), and tougher monsters for levels 25 to 55.

Build 248

  • Greenwood is open. Take the Kingsway north from Mistmarsh to an old forest and the foresters' town of Thornwick, with its own map quest, two Guard's Rests and a Roots statue for Etching.
  • Elderwood trees (Woodcutting 60), a salmon run, and tougher monsters for levels 20 to 45.
  • Opening the region map from inside a building now shows the town around you.

Build 243

  • Step inside. Ten buildings now open into rooms, including the Salted Eel, several forges and banks, the Wayside, the Leaky Eel, the Windy Ewe and Mother Gloam's hut. Tap one and choose Go into….

Build 237

  • Controls hints are now separate Tip: lines instead of being spoken by people, and quest tips stay in the Journal under that step.
  • A Bird's-Eye View in Highmoor has a new step.

Build 233

Build 230

  • Taps are more forgiving: tap anywhere on a person's, monster's or item pile's hex to talk, attack or pick up.
  • New look for the compass, health heart and day dial, plus wooden signposts you can tap to read.
  • × buttons to close Magic, the Journal and the long-press card. Level-up banners now sit above everything else.

Build 226

  • Brewing in Mistmarsh. Mother Gloam's cauldron, a new herb, two new draughts, and a new quest from Tobin in Reedwick. See Brewing.
  • Marsh fever. Some marsh creatures can make you ill. There's a cure, and Gloam sells a tonic.
