How monsters behave

  • Aggressive monsters attack when you come close. Passive ones only fight back.
  • A monster chases you until it's 7 hexes from home. Then it gives up, walks back and heals to full.
  • Defeated monsters come back after a while.
  • Loot falls on the ground and stays for 2 minutes.
  • Some monsters only come out at night, others only by day. At dawn or dusk they slip away, unless they're fighting you.
  • Every monster has a small chance, about 1 in 100, to drop a weapon.
  • Any monster of level 5 or higher can drop a worn riddle map, 1 time in 120. See Riddle maps.

Monster list

For Archery, some monsters are armoured (arrows do a quarter less) and some light (a quarter more); the monster card shows the tag. Armoured: Bone Guard sentries, golems, the Hollow warden, sentries and sentinels, the Gate-breaker, the Starsteel champion, deep knights, seal colossi, greyed guards and the Bone Captain. Most bandits, wolfmen, wreckers, raiders and drakes are light.
