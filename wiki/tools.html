Tools and the tool belt

Axes, pickaxes, rods, hammers and chisels hang on your belt, out of your bag. Better tools work faster.

The tool belt

Hattie gives you a tool belt early on Saltmere, along with a wooden axe, a wooden pickaxe and a driftwood rod. Until you have it, gathering tells you: "You'll need tools for that. Maybe someone on the strand can help."

  • The belt has five loops: Woodcutting (axe), Mining (pickaxe), Fishing (rod), Smithing (hammer) and Etching (chisel).
  • Tools on the belt don't take bag slots.
  • Use a better tool from your bag and it goes on the belt; your old one drops back into your bag so you can sell it.
  • Each tool needs a level in its own skill.

The first time you use a forge without a hammer, the smith lends you a wooden mallet.

Faster with better tools

A better axe, pickaxe or rod raises your chance of success on every try. The bonus multiplies your chance: +100% means twice as likely, up to a sure thing.

The number in brackets is the level needed in that tool's skill. A crafted sturdy axe also gives +25%.

Wyrmfang tools (Woodcutting or Mining 70 to use, smithed at Smithing 78 from 1 wyrmfang and 2 hexsteel bars) give +250%, and each has a trick:

  • The wyrmfang axe sometimes chars the cut, and a lump of coal drops out with the log (about 1 in 20). Sells for 5,400.
  • The wyrmfang pickaxe sometimes smelts the ore as it comes out, so you get the bar instead, with half the smelting XP (about 1 in 20).

Metal axes and pickaxes are smithed; see Smithing. Rods past ash, and heartsteel tools, exist but can't be made yet from what the world gives you.
