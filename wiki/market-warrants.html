How warrants work

Each of the eight markets keeps one warrant up at a time: a big order for one thing, picked to suit your skill levels. The warrant sheet hangs on the general store, with a delivery crate beside it. (At the Grand Market it's the guild hall, once the hall is restored. See Town works.)

  1. Tap the crate to see the order: what, how many, what it pays and how many days are left.
  2. If you want it, choose Take this warrant. A taken warrant has no clock (see below).
  3. Deliver from your bag, a bit at a time if you like. A Kingsway runner or post rook can deliver straight to the crate too.
  4. Once it's filled, the crate is nailed shut and the sheet stamped FILLED. The pay goes to your bank.

A warrant pays about 2.5 times shop value (2.2 times for things up to level 30), plus Trade XP (0.1 per coin), XP in the skill that made the goods (10% of the making XP) and a Market Seal. It's sized to take about fifty minutes.

A warrant you haven't taken stays up for 3 real days, counted from the first time you read its card. Fill it and a new one goes up the next day. If it runs out part-done, you're still paid for what you delivered, with no penalty.

Taking a warrant

Any warrant can be taken from its crate card. A taken warrant never runs out: it waits until you fill it or give it up, and that market posts nothing new in the meantime. You can hold one taken warrant per market, at as many markets as you like.

Taken warrants show in the Journal under their province, with your count so far ("3 of 140 raw shrimp"), the pay, where the crate is, a Pin button and Give it up. Giving up pays for what you've delivered so far, the same as a warrant that runs out, and the market posts a new one the next day.

Townsfolk post these orders, so you'll see familiar names on them. Brannoc and the other smiths post their smithing orders this way. Fine pieces from a village forge count. See The village forges.

The Market Clerks' chest

Fill any 5 warrants in 7 days and the clerks send you a chest: 25,000 coins and 3 Market Seals. You can earn it at most once a week.

Spending Market Seals

Tap a delivery crate and choose Spend Market Seals. Everything here is for your The Driftwood Cottage, so patch the cottage roof first.
