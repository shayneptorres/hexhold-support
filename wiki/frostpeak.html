At a glance

High, snowbound mountains under Bellstone Peak, which rings like a bell when the wind is right. The town is Kettlepass, a Delvers' town that lives because of its hot spring. The toughest province so far: good for levels 35 to 65.

Places: Kettlepass, the Switchbacks and the Delvings, the Fells, the tarn, the Rimewood, the Barrows, the Shoulder and Hornhold, and the North Gate. The mountains climb in steps here, up to five levels high, and the higher and further out you go, the tougher it gets.

Getting here: the Cold Road, east from Greenwood or west from Cinderreach. Or ride the mine cart from Emberfall, by day only: "The carters won't drive the Cold Road in the dark."

Around the mountains

AreaWhat's there
The SwitchbacksThe road from Kettlepass up three cliff lines to the Delvings door, high on its shelf under Bellstone Peak. Mind the edges.
The FellsHigh bare ground in the north, with ice spires that sing in the wind. Herders say the old clans used the spires' notes to find their way in a whiteout.
The tarnA frozen lake on a shelf to the north-east, with the ice-fishers. The tarn track climbs to it from the Cold Road.
The RimewoodThick snow pines in the south-west, with snow wolves, the hunters' lodge, and the Black Tarn, a small lake that never freezes.
The BarrowsOld clan barrows on the high ground in the south-west. "Leave them be after dark."
The ShoulderA great massif in the south-east. A long track climbs it to Hornhold, a broken watchtower on top, with a view of all Frostpeak. Rams on the ledges, bears in the crags, and a giant who sits up there by day.

People

Reeve Halde (the town's reeve), Gunna Delve (foreman of the Delvers), Sorrel (keeps the Kettle) and Sergeant Brask (guards the road south).

Sorrel gives this region's map quest, The Singing Map. See Maps.

Danger

The Bone Guard walk here in force, and they aren't the only thing in the snow: wolves in the Rimewood, cragbears, and what walks out of the Barrows at night. See Monsters.

The night cold: out in the open snow at night, the cold wears you down. Get indoors or drink a Frostward. See Harsh places.

Mending house: a warm bath at the Kettle. Fee 500.

Guard's Rest: the North Gate rest, furred with rime. See Bones and the Guard's Rest.

The Delvings

The old Delver mine under Bellstone Peak. Gunna Delve keeps its gate, and she's the one to talk to.

Spoiler Reveals: what's below, once you're let in (no quest answers)
  • Gunna's quests open the mine's first floor, the Upper Works, and she gives you her lamp.
  • It's dark down there. On your own you see about 2 hexes. Carry the lamp in your bag to see further. Tap a wall torch and choose Light the torch; it stays lit for good. Monster health bars only show where there's light.
  • The lift remembers every landing you've ridden to.
  • A side quest, The Buckled Line, mends the cart line under the mountain. After that the carts run day and night, with a stop inside the mine, and a station with more than one stop asks where you're going.
  • Fall down there and you wake at the Kettle, with your grave left below. Sorrel can fetch your things back for 10,000 coins.
Spoiler Reveals: the deep floors and what waits at the bottom (no puzzle answers)
  • Below the Upper Works are two deeper floors and, at the very bottom, the Hold, behind the Bell Door.
  • The deep floors have their own Guard's watch. Lay it to rest like any other and the knights down there stand aside for good.
  • Something big lives in the Hold. When the floor starts to crack, move. What the Bell clan left behind helps.
  • Fall in the Hold and your grave is left just outside its door.
  • Seeing it through earns you something every province guards, and a new spell.
