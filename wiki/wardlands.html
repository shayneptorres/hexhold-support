What the islanders will tell you

You wake on a grey beach on Saltmere Isle, a windy fishing island off the coast of the mainland, with no idea how you got there.

Ask around and you'll hear about the Wardlands: six provinces in a ring, joined by an old royal road called the Kingsway. At the centre of the ring is a wall of mountains, the Crownwall, and behind it a keep with six walls, the Hexhold. Its gates have been shut for as long as anyone can remember.

Lately, skeletons in old royal armour have started walking out of the ruins. Nobody on the island agrees on why.

HexSeal's story is told in pieces, by different people, and not everyone tells it the same way. Talk to everyone, and read the signposts.

About this wiki and spoilers

This wiki explains how the game works. It keeps the story for the game to tell.

  • Never here: story twists and secrets, quest solutions, riddle answers, spell recipes and hidden places.
  • Behind a tap: things you'd normally discover by playing, such as the spells waiting in your spellbook. They show as a dashed Spoiler box that says what it reveals, and search never shows what's inside.
  • Open: how things work, and anything the game shows you up front.

Names of places and people are working names and may change.
