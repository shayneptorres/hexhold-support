At a glance

Hot, red badlands under the Kiln, a smoking volcano whose plume you can see from the whole east. The town is Emberfall, home of the hottest furnace in the Wardlands. Good for levels 25 to 55.

Places: Emberfall, the Kiln, the Scorch, the Glass Sands, the Steamwells, the old workings, the foothills and the North-East Gate. The further you walk from Emberfall, the tougher it gets.

Getting here: take the Kingsway north out of Highmoor. You come in at the south edge and follow the road north to Emberfall on the coast.

What's here

Services: a bank in a red tower, a general store (shut at night), the Quench tavern, a glassworks, the Anvil Council hall, and a Roadwarden post box (deposit only: whatever you drop in turns up in your bank).

Waystone: Emberfall, and another near the North-East Gate.

Around the Reach

AreaWhat's there
The ScorchA raised red mesa in the north-west, rich in coal, iron and gold. Slag crawlers tunnel through it. The Scorch track climbs to an old Delver lookout on its knoll, with a view over the whole Reach.
The Glass SandsRed dunes west of town. Dune vipers lie just under the sand.
The SteamwellsTwo hot springs and steam vents out west by the Ashfall, with a bathhouse. Emberfall smiths say if the vents ever stop together, run.
The old workingsSlag heaps and fallen-in huts from Delver mines shut long ago. After dark, embers drift between the heaps with nothing burning under them.
The FirebreakAn old warm crack running west from the Kiln toward the Scorch, with small lava pools.
The foothillsThe road south-west to the North-East Gate, past the fireclay pits.

People

Forgemistress Ysolde (head of the Anvil Council), Tamsy Glass (glassblower), Cass (salamander handler, with a scorched little skiff) and Old Bellows (a retired smith).

Tamsy gives this region's map quest, The Glass Map. See Maps.

Danger

Raiders work the roads, and their warchief keeps the drum on the Cold Road by day. Ash hounds run round the town's edges, the Bone Guard keep a watch here too, and golems walk the Kiln itself. See Monsters.

Mending house: Old Bellows' burn shed, behind the great furnace. Fee 250.

Guard's Rest: the Kiln road rest, baked red. See Bones and the Guard's Rest.

Gold doesn't come easy. A vein gives one ore and then needs time to come back. The Kiln's heat wears you down while you're on it; a Cinderward keeps it off. See Harsh places.
