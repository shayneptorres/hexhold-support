Mags the Pedlar

A wandering pedlar who sets up in a different town each day, with four rare things for sale.

pedlarmagscuriosledgershopsroads-tilesriddle-maps

Finding her

Mags travels with a cart. She turns up once you've finished your first days on Saltmere.

  • Each in-game day (20 real minutes), she stands near the general store in one town.
  • She only visits towns you've been to, in order: Saltmere village, Kingsferry, Reedwick, Windle, Thornwick, Emberfall, Kettlepass.
  • Not sure where she is? Every notice board says which town she's in today.

What she sells

Talk to her and choose What have you got?

  • Four things a day, one of each, at a fixed price.
  • What she brings depends on the regions you've reached.
  • Miss something and it may come back another day.

Her wares include curios, rare keepsakes from around the provinces that only she sells, plus bundles of Roads tiles for Magic and worn riddle maps (Riddle maps). Curios you buy go into your Ledger.

Curios cost thousands of coins. Fill a few notice-board orders before you go looking for her.

More curios from provinces not built yet, plus dyes, hats, lanterns and cottage furniture.
