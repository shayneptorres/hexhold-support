Bones and the Guard's Rest

Lay the Bone Guard's bones to rest and each province calms down. Bones also make ash, broth and coins.

Laying the Guard to rest

The Bone Guard are skeletons in old royal armour. Every province has a Guard's Rest: a roll stone with a lantern, where their bones can be laid to rest. Greenwood has two, Rootwatch and Greywatch, and both count toward Greenwood. Cinderreach's is the Kiln road rest, and Frostpeak's is the North Gate rest.

  • Tap the rest with Guard bones and choose to lay one, five, or all. You kneel and set each one down, and each takes a tick: "Your watch is ended."
  • Bones count toward the province the rest is in, wherever the bones came from.
  • Tap the stone to see how far along the province is and how many more bones the next step needs.

The Guard's Blessing

Lay 5 or more at once and you get the Guard's Blessing: the Bone Guard hit you 5% less often for 10 minutes. A badge shows on screen. Laying more starts the 10 minutes again.

Steps

As you lay bones, each province moves through five steps: Restless, Uneasy, Settled, At peace and Honoured. Saltmere needs fewer bones than the other provinces. Tap a rest to see where you are.

At Uneasy, the Guard leave you alone if your combat level is 10 or more above theirs. What the later steps bring is for you to find out.

Spoiler Reveals: what changes at Settled, At peace and Honoured
  • Settled: the Guard stand down and stop starting fights, and the province changes.
  • At peace: the lantern is lit. The rest heals you fully, once every 20 minutes, and some monsters drop tiles more often.
  • Honoured: a keepsake from the province.

The Guard's Rest is introduced by a hidden quest on Saltmere. Keep your ears open for rumours. See Hidden quests.

Spoiler Reveals: a spell that helps with this

The Lay to Rest spell (Magic 30) lays every Guard bone in your bag to rest from wherever you are in that province, at half value. You have to discover it first. See Magic.

The Captain's Rest

In the Crown Valley, the Captain's Rest is a memorial to the Crown Watch's officers, where their captains' bones are laid. Where those bones are is for you to find.

Other uses

  • Bone ash. Burn bones at a forge (Smithing 1): bones make 1 ash, big bones 3, Guard bones 2, dragon bones 6. Ash with hexslate makes extra wardglass for Etching.
  • Fish broth. Cook 1 raw fish with 2 bones or 1 big bones. See Cooking.
  • Selling. Ada Pike in Fairmeadow buys bones for 4 coins each and big bones for 12.
  • Trading. Someone in Mistmarsh will trade magic tiles for Guard bones.

Bone crafting (needles, hooks, beads, dice and charms), and more kinds of bones.
