How quests work

  • Quests start by talking to people. Someone with work for you will offer it once you're ready, and ask if you'll take it on. Say no and they won't keep asking: the offer waits at the top of their talk menu for whenever you're ready, and you still hear the whole story.
  • A story card opens when a quest starts (brown ribbon), gets a new step (blue) or is complete (gold, with your rewards). It shows who's speaking, with their portrait, and the world waits while you read. Tap Next, then Done.
  • Quest talk follows what you say. Some pages give you two replies to pick from.
  • Steps can mean talking to people, bringing things, beating monsters, or inspecting places and objects. A normal tap does it when a quest wants you to look at something; long-pressing works too.
  • Lost? Talk to the quest giver again. Once you've taken a quest, they tell you where things stand.
  • When a quest is done, its giver has one last word for you, said once.
  • Anyone a quest is waiting on stays out at their usual spot after dark and will still talk to you. Shops still close at night.
  • If you have the region's map, a red ! marks anyone with a step for you.

HexSeal's quests are puzzles. They send you from place to place and expect you to look closely. This page won't spoil them.

The Journal

Open Journal from the dock. A gold dot on it, and a short Journal updated message, mean something new has been written. The Talk to chip, and the pinned quest line above the dock, show the person's portrait.

Quests

Your quests, grouped by region with the one you're in first. Each shows how far along you are, your latest diary line, who to see and where, and live counts of anything you're collecting. If a step needs a particular control, a Tip: line under the diary entry tells you how, for example to press and hold a hex to look closer. Finished quests fold away under Done. If you're carrying a riddle map, its riddle is listed here too (never the answer).

Rumours

Every story quest is listed here from the start, under the region it begins in, with its name and where it starts, so you always know where to go next. People also let rumours slip the first time you talk to them, and the innkeeper at the Salted Eel will tell you one you haven't heard if you ask "Any news?".

A few quests show only as ??? until you hear their rumour. Each region's header counts the quests you know and the ones still ???. See Hidden quests.

If you'd rather see every quest start, hidden ones included, turn on Journal: show all quest starts in Settings.

Maps

The region maps you own, and a hint about where to find the ones you don't.

Notice-board orders and town works as errands, long-press to pin, rereading finished quests, and a ribbon for finishing a region.

Spoilers

None here, on purpose. If you're stuck, try tapping or long-pressing everything near where the quest points you, and check the time of day.
