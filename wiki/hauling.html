Sacks, chests and bundles

Carry more than 28 things. Belt sacks catch overflow, field chests hold your haul on site, bundles pack many items into one slot, and the King's Post and its rooks carry loads to the bank.

Gathering sacks

Make sacks at a workbench with Crafting. A sack hangs on your tool belt and takes no bag slot; tap it in your bag to hang it.

  • Each sack holds one kind of thing: a log sling for logs, a fish creel for raw fish, an ore sack for ore.
  • When your bag is full, what you gather goes into the sack on its own.
  • Fill on the Pack tab moves matching items from your bag into the sack.
  • Deposit all at a bank empties your sacks too.
  • A bigger sack replaces a smaller one once the old one is empty.

Field chests

A field chest is a big box you set down where you're working.

  1. Craft one, then tap Set down on its card while standing by an open hex. Chests can't go in towns, on roads or on travel points.
  2. Tap the chest to open it. Stow all empties your bag into it. Everything stacks inside.
  3. Pick it up when you're done. An empty chest is just a chest; a full one becomes a loaded chest that holds the whole load in one bag slot.
  4. Deposit the loaded chest at any bank to unpack it.

A loaded chest halves your walking speed, except the light willow hamper.

Bundles

From Crafting 10, tie raw logs, fish or ore with 1 twine at a workbench into a bundle that takes one bag slot. Bundling gives no XP.

The King's Post

The Roadwardens once ran a post along the Kingsway. Old Hob in Kingsferry still has the horn. Help him with The Last Toll and the Kingsferry post house opens again, and the post horn goes on your tool belt.

Calling a runner

Tap the post horn in your Pack and a Kingsway runner comes to you. Choose what he carries:

  • My bag: your resources (logs, fish, ore and the like). Tools, gear, belt kit and quest things stay with you. Bundles go too.
  • My sacks: whatever your gathering sacks hold.
  • To today's warrant: a runner or rook can deliver straight to a market's warrant crate. See Market Warrants.
  • My ore to the forge: your ore and coal go to the village forges' shared ore bin instead of the bank. See The village forges. Rooks can carry ore there too.

The load lands in your bank about a minute later. If the bank is full, it waits at the post house until you make room.

FeeAbout a tenth of the load's shop value, at least 5 coins, paid from your bag
Farther awayHalf again for each province between you and the nearest open post house
How oftenOnce every 5 minutes

Runners keep to the roads. They won't come underground, indoors, out to Saltmere, or near the gates in the Crownwall. A runner also needs an open post house somewhere he can reach.

Every province on the Kingsway had a post house once. Ask around in each town.

Post boxes

Roadwarden post boxes stand on Saltmere and along the mainland woods and roads. They are deposit only: whatever goes in the slot turns up in your bank, free. Not every box still works.

Nails and signs

  • Iron nails: forge, Smithing 10, 1 iron bar makes 10 (12.5 XP).
  • Signboard: workbench, Crafting 8, 2 planks and 5 iron nails (30 XP).

Post rooks

Ida on Saltmere keeps rooks, and she hears everything. Talk to her at her stall to start Ida's Rumour Mill; it ends with a rook whistle on your tool belt.

  1. Tap the whistle in your Pack.
  2. Pick one stack: one kind of thing, up to 28.
  3. The rook takes your cheapest fish as its fee (raw or cooked) and flies the stack to your bank about half a minute later.
  • Rooks fly from anywhere outdoors, Saltmere and the gate stones included. Not indoors, and never underground.
  • Each rook rests 3 minutes after a flight. You start with one rook, get a second at Crafting 30 and a third at Crafting 60.
  • No fish, no flight. If the stack you're sending is fish, the rook is paid with a different fish.
  • Ida's rooks roost on the Saltmere mill.

Spoiler Reveals: a late-game way to bank from the field

Once all six post houses are open, and with Crafting 85 and Etching 70, Old Hob's ledger has one more entry for you. It leads to the Kingsbox: a strongbox you set down like a field chest. Whatever you put in goes to your bank when you close the lid, for 1 Roads tile per 50 items. Without enough tiles, it all waits in the box.
