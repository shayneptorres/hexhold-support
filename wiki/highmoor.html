Wide, windy moorland, fells, tarns and windmills around the hill town of Windle.

At a glance

Wide high moorland around Windle, a shepherds' hill town, with windmills on the ridges. Good for levels 10 to 35.

It's windy up here: gusts sweep across the sky in bursts, and the windmill sails spin faster when a gust comes through. Grass, heather and trees sway with it. For a stiller moor, turn on Calmer effects in Settings.

Places: Windle, the Fells, moorland tarns, the Sixstones (a ring of standing stones), Hollin Wood, the foothills and the East Gate. A ruined tower, the Windwarden tower, stands on the north moor. See The Windwarden tower.

Waystones: Windle and the East Gate.

The Windwarden tower

A track off the Kingsway leads up to a ruined tower on the north moor. Inside are four floors, each harder than the last. Words are carved into the walls, and they're worth reading closely.

Folk in Windle say something penned up on the roof still howls, and that whoever quiets it will find a glide home waiting.

If you fall in the tower, you wake at Bryn's shed in Windle.

People

Wren (the glider-maker's daughter), Bryn Hollis (head of the shepherd clans), Morwen Hollis (the smith), Old Corra, and Captain Hale Yarrow, who runs the archery butts and starts First Feather. See Archery.

Danger

Moorland creatures roam the open moor: fell rams, heath adders, crag kites and tor trolls, and people say a barrow wight walks among the cairns at night. Bandits by day, a highwayman by night, the Bone Guard, and after dark a moor wolfman (level 24) prowling Hollin Wood that attacks from 4 hexes away. Heed the north signpost. See Monsters.

At night the general store closes.

Mending house: the shepherds' lambing shed. Fee 60.
