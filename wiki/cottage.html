The Driftwood Cottage

Your home on Saltmere. Fix it up room by room for a bed, a hearth and a larder.

cottagehomehousingsaltmerelardersleep

Getting the cottage

Once you've finished your first days on Saltmere, Hattie lets you the Driftwood Cottage for free. It's a wreck, so you'll be paying a carpenter to fix it up.

Tap the cottage to see inside, the rooms you can pay for, and buttons for Sleep, Cook and Larder once you have them. Rooms are paid for with coins from your bag.

Rooms

Patch the roof comes first. After that, buy the rest in any order.

RoomCostWhat it gives
Patch the roof500A bed. Sleep at night to skip to morning and heal to full. Sleeping here once makes it where you wake after a fall on Saltmere, for free. You can't sleep while something is fighting or chasing you close behind.
Hearth and range2,500Cook at home, just like a fire.
Larder chest5,000Larder: 50 slots where everything stacks. It opens only at the cottage, separate from the bank.
Trophy wall15,000A wall for the things you're proudest of.

Once the roof is patched, Market Seals buy furnishings too: a framed warrant, a rug, a rocking chair, a floor lantern, a dresser and a banner by the door. See Spending Market Seals.

More rooms (a workshop, a map room, a garden, a hall for your curios and a second floor), trophies to hang, and a larder you can reach from the bank.
