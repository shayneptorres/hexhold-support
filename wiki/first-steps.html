What HexSeal is

HexSeal is an offline adventure for iPhone in the spirit of Old School RuneScape. The world is a board of hexes. You tap where you want to go, and everything happens on a steady game tick of 0.6 seconds. You train skills, fight, trade, and slowly piece together what's happening in the Wardlands.

There are no classes. What you become is down to what you do.

The title screen

  • Continue picks up your last save and shows its name.
  • New Game starts a fresh character in an empty save slot.
  • Saves lists up to five saves. See Save slots.
  • Hard mode is a checkbox you tick before New Game. In Hard mode, falling in battle loses what you carry. See Death and the mending house.

Making your character

On a new game you're asked: "Who washed ashore?" Choose hair, beard, moustache, eyes, eyepatch, brows, nose, skin, colours, top, legs and clothes dye. Surprise me picks everything at random.

You can change your look any time from Settings › Change look…

Washed Ashore

You wake on a grey beach on Saltmere Isle, soaked and cold. Hattie, a beachcomber, gives you an empty tool belt and tells you the island's folk will fill it.

  1. First, Wenna Gale the harbourmaster gives you a practice sword and some bread and shows you how to fight.
  2. Then visit the island's mentors, in any order:

  1. Show Hattie your full belt to finish. You get 25 coins, and each lesson you complete gives 25 XP in its skill.

While you're learning, falling in a fight on Saltmere costs you nothing.

Mentors remember where you got to, so you can wander off mid-lesson and pick it up later. Brannoc stays at his forge at any hour until you've finished your lessons, and Hattie will tell you which jobs are still left.

The first drowned oarsman you beat always drops a Waters tile, so you have one to practise with after Tamsin's magic lesson.

Skipping

  • One lesson at a time. Every mentor offers "Teach me" (a small job and the lesson XP) or "I know the trade" (the tool straight away, no job, no XP).
  • The whole thing. On the very first card, tap "I've been to Saltmere before", then hold Hold to skip for two seconds. You get every tool, the sword, the starter tiles and the coins, and wake in the village.

After that

Hattie has a few ideas about where to go next, and the islanders have more. Where you go first is up to you. See Saltmere Isle and Quests.
