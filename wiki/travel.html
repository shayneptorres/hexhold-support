Walking

Each region is its own map. Walk to an exit at its edge to travel on; the game shows "You set off for …" and loads the next region.

Long-press an exit that won't open to see why:

  • "The road beyond is not yet passable." The region beyond isn't in the game yet.
  • "Sealed. It won't budge." One of the Crownwall's gates, not open to you yet. See The Crown Valley.

Gliders

Spoiler Reveals: a way to travel you unlock

There are gliders between Windle and Kingsferry. They open up through one of the hidden quests, so listen for rumours.

The ferry

The ferry between Saltmere and Kingsferry sails by day only. On Saltmere it has to be built first: Pell needs 25 oak logs and 10 bronze bars. You can bring them over several trips, and she tells you how far along you are.

Waystones

Most towns have a waystone. Touch one and "it hums, and you feel it remember you." A waystone you've touched is a place your teleport spells can take you.
