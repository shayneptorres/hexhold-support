Mistmarsh

A wide, foggy marsh of reed beds, bog and black meres around the stilt village of Reedwick.

At a glance

A big, foggy marsh, about the size of Fairmeadow, with plenty of open water and bog. Reedwick sits on stilts at its heart, and its houses thin out into the reeds the further you go. Good for levels 10 to 35.

Places: Reedwick, the Bell, Willow Carr, the Meres (a boardwalk causeway across the water), the foothills and the West Gate. Somewhere out in the marsh, the old folk say, an older village went under.

There's no forge here.

Services: a bank barge, a general store, the Leaky Eel, and Mother Gloam's chest of reedsilk robes and her fever tonic.

Waystones: Reedwick and the West Gate.

People

Tobin Reed (reed-cutter and unofficial mayor), Marta Eelwife (sells eels), and Mother Gloam (the bog witch).

Danger

Fen toads sit near the village. Further out you'll meet bog-drowned, mire golems and Bone Guard scouts, and the deeper you go into the marsh, the tougher its creatures get. See Monsters. After dark, bog-drowned gather on the road to the West Gate.

Bog-drowned and mire golems can give you marsh fever. See Marsh fever for the cures.

Mending house: Mother Gloam's hut. Fee 60.

At night the general store and Marta's eel trade close. Windows, the bank barge and the eel pools glow, and the Drowned Bell rings at midnight.
