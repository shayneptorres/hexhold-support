Town works

Pay to fix up the towns. Each repair changes the town for good and opens a lasting perk.

How it works

Some townsfolk want something fixed. Talk to them and pick Fund. Each work costs coins plus materials, taken from your bag first, then your bank.

When it's done, the town changes on the map and a perk opens for good. Some works only appear after another work, or after you've made progress in the town's story.

Fund every work in a region to earn its Patron title.

Late works

The biggest works of all are for players deep into the game, and cost a fortune.

Spoiler Reveals: the late works in the Crown Valley and Greenwood
  • The Lantern hall (ask Ide at the Lantern camp): her tent becomes a timber hall with a free bed to wake in, a range, and a once-a-day top-up for your part-drunk bottles. Fall in the valley and her carriers bring your grave to the hall.
  • The spoke lanterns: the camp waystone can send you out along the valley's spokes.
  • The duel-ring stands: a crowd for your duels, a banner for each tier you've won, a trophy for your cottage, and re-fights for practice.
  • Six Crownwall beacons, one beside each gate you've walked through. Each lets the camp waystone send you out to that province, and its glow shows on the province's skyline. Light all six to be called Beaconkeeper.
  • The King's grove in Thornwick (ask Hesper Vane): a ring of young elders, the title Grovewarden, and a cutting from Hesper for the garden by your The Driftwood Cottage, which grows slowly over the real weeks.

More works in every town (a bridge, a barber, lodgings, a glider launch, a dye shed, a bathhouse and more), perks for the big works above, and Patron titles for every region.
