Fairmeadow

Farmland on the river Brin and the trading port of Kingsferry, home of the Grand Market.

At a glance

Rolling farmland around the port town of Kingsferry, with 18 farmsteads in the fields and gentle terraces rising from the harbour to the South Gate. Four bridges cross the river Brin. It's the biggest province on the mainland. Good for levels 5 to 25.

Places: Kingsferry and its cobbled market square, the farms, the foothills, the Old Mill, Spire Hill (an old ruin on top you can see from far off), the tollhouse ruins and the South Gate.

Services: a bank, a general store, Silas Quill's curio shop (tiles, robes, riddle maps), a tavern (bed for 5 coins), the docks, a lighthouse, the old post house (ask Old Hob) and a Roadwarden post box on the Spire Hill road. The Old Mill grinds wheat and sells baking things: flour, jugs, eggs, milk, apples and raw meat, plus leather and tallow for archer's armour. Flax grows in the fields, and the farmsteads have spinning wheels and hen runs (for feathers). Wheat grows in the fields and bread ovens stand in the farmsteads. See Baking.

The Grand Market: three stalls and a place for your own, once they're fixed up. See The Grand Market and Trade and your stall.

Livestock: sheep and cows graze in fenced pens by the farmsteads, with a few strays near Kingsferry. They never fight back, and they give raw meat, fleece and cowhide.

Waystones: the Old Mill and the South Gate.

People

Ferryman Odo, Old Hob (the tollkeeper), Silas Quill (curio dealer), Ada Pike (runs the Old Mill), Fen Barrow (a barge-master with a broken barge), Lorna Wick (lighthouse keeper), Aggie Brand (the smith), Nell, a mender who walks the Kingsway at dusk, and Prue Crock, a potter across the Brin from the watermill, who teaches pottery.

Danger

Kingsway bandits by day, highwaymen by night, and Bone Guard scouts in the foothills after dark. See Monsters.

Kingsferry folk say the old Guard still keep the gate, up on the South Gate terrace. Go carefully.

Mending house: the Wayside Mending House, behind the Kingsferry tavern. Fee 25.

Guard's Rest: the Tollhouse rest, by the tollhouse ruins. See Bones and the Guard's Rest.
