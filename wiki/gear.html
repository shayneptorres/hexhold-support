Your gear on the Pack tab

Open Pack from the dock. Your gear sits above your bag: a paper doll with seven slots around a 3D model of your hero that you can spin:

  • Left: Head, Cape, Body, Legs
  • Right: Weapon, Shield, Hands

Below are your total Attack, Strength and Defence bonuses (with the change from your last swap), your combat level, the attack style buttons and your tool belt.

  • Tap an item in your bag to wear it.
  • Tap a worn slot to take it off. You need room in your bag.

Weapons and shields show in your hero's hands, and armour shows on your hero in its metal's colour.

Each metal above steel adds a little more again; an embersteel sword is 26 / 23, a deepsteel one 44 / 38. Slow weapons hit harder per swing; fast ones hit more often. Staffs are crafted from logs and are mostly for Magic.

Archer's armour

Light leather for Archery. It needs Defence and Archery at its level, and adds to your Archery hit chance as well as your Defence. Bows take both hands, so there's no shield.

Drake gear

Made from what the drakes of Wyrmholm leave behind. Drakehide and Wyrmscale are crafted at a workbench; wyrmfang pieces are smithed at a forge.

  • Drakehide is light, so it doesn't throw off spellcasting the way metal does.
  • The drakehide body and Wyrmscale body keep off Wyrmholm's heat and halve drake breath. The Wyrmscale shield takes a quarter off breath.
  • The wyrmfang spear hits from 2 hexes away. It sells for 5,000 coins.
  • For the wyrmglass staff see Mage gear, and for the wyrmfang axe and pickaxe see Tools and the tool belt.

Heartsteel, the metal above hexsteel, is in the game's data but needs an ore you can't mine yet.
