Woodcutting

Chop trees for logs. Logs feed Crafting (staffs, rods, sacks, chests) and twine.

How to chop

Tap a tree. You walk next to it and swing every 4 ticks (2.4 seconds). Each swing has a chance to give logs, and that chance rises with your level and your axe. After giving logs, a tree may fall; it grows back after a short while.

You keep chopping until your bag is full, the tree falls, or you tap elsewhere. A full bag spills into a log sling on your belt if you've made one (see Crafting).

Extras

  • About 1 log in 150 comes with a Roots tile for Magic.
  • Some Magic can help here, once you've found it.

Every Woodcutting level band now has a tree.

Frostpine

Tall pines frosted blue-white, up in Frostpeak. Folk in Kettlepass say the best stands are out in the Rimewood and up on the Shoulder.

Heartwood

The rarest tree in the Wardlands. Folk say there's one by every Warden in the land, and you'll know it by its fruit, which glows softly. A heartwood gives about six logs before it's down to a stump, and it takes about half an hour to grow back. When one is ready again, word reaches you wherever you are: "A heartwood is ready in …"

Muscle Memory stops once the heartwood you were chopping is down.
