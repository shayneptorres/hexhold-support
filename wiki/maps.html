Maps

Each region's map has to be earned. The world map stays under cloud until you do.

Opening the map

Tap the compass or the place name at the top, or open ··· › Map. The map has two tabs.

The region map

If you have this region's map, you see a parchment chart you can drag and pinch. Tap a mark for its card.

Open it from inside a building and it shows the town outside.

If you don't, you see fog and a hint about who keeps the chart, such as "Reedwick folk say Tobin Reed keeps the only chart."

The Wardlands map

The whole realm. Provinces you have no map for stay under cloud, but your own position always shows as a pulsing gold ring. The footer counts your maps, out of 7.

Earning maps

Each region's map comes from a short map quest given by someone who lives there. See Quests. Maps aren't bag items; you keep them for good.

Until you have it, the region's map page tells you what to do next: finish an earlier quest first, who to ask, or that the map quest is already in your Journal.

When you earn one, that province's cloud rolls back and its mark on the compass lights up.
