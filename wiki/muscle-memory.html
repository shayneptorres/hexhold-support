Muscle Memory

Once you know a skill well enough, your character keeps working at it while the game is closed, slower than playing.

Switching it on

Muscle Memory starts switched off. The first time one of your skills reaches its Muscle Memory level, the person who taught you that skill sends for you, and their quest switches it on. Finish it once and every skill has Muscle Memory from then on.

SkillMentor
WoodcuttingPell
FishingGaffer Moss
Mining, SmithingBrannoc
Cooking, EtchingOld Tamsin
CraftingHattie Combe

Until you've finished it, the dark hands chip points you to the quest: it shows what to do next without a tap, and the mentor gets a red ! on your region map.

After that, a skill's mentor has a word for you when it unlocks Muscle Memory and when it reaches each new tier, and the card shows what the new tier does. The quest's steps are in your Journal; this wiki won't spoil them.

How it works

Start gathering or making something, then leave the app: go to the home screen, lock your phone, or switch apps. Your character keeps working, at a slower pace than if you were playing. When you come back, the game works out what you made using the same chances, burns and XP as normal play.

  • Always slower than playing. It's a bonus, not a replacement.
  • Away for less than 30 seconds? Nothing happens.
  • It stops when your storage is full, you run out of materials, or after 12 hours.
  • It never runs in a fight or with something hostile coming for you. The fight waits exactly as you left it, and the card says "Your hands stopped: you were under attack."
  • It doesn't run while bundling either.
  • Cooking, Smithing and Crafting only finish the batch you chose.

Tiers

Your level in the skill you're training sets how well your hands keep going.

TierLevelPaceWhat fills up
The Knack1025%Your bag
Habit3035%Your bag and your sacks
Routine5045%Plus a field chest within 2 hexes
Second Nature7055%Breaks of up to 2 minutes count at full pace
Mastery9065%–

Making skills start at level 15 (Etching at 20), so their first tier, The Knack, begins there. The later tiers open at the same levels.

Make a sack and set down a field chest before you leave. More room means more hours of work. See Sacks, chests and bundles.

The hands chip

A small hands chip sits under the place name.

  • Lit, with a note like "Bag full in ~16 min": leaving now would count.
  • Dark: it wouldn't.

Tap it to see why. When it's dark, it tells you the reason, for example that wardglass smelting is Etching work and only counts from Etching 20.

While you were away

When you come back, a While you were away card shows your hero, how long you were gone, how long your hands worked, why they stopped, the items, XP and levels you gained, and any draught sips you used.
