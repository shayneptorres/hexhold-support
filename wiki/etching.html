Etching

Make your own magic tiles. Mine hexslate, smelt it into wardglass, and etch it at a Warden statue.

Step by step

  1. Mine hexslate. It's in every region, needs Mining 1, and never runs out. See Mining.
  2. Smelt it at a forge into blank wardglass. No level needed: 1 tick, 2 Etching XP.
  3. Walk to a Warden statue. Long-press it and tap Etch. Each blank becomes tiles of that statue's element. If a monster hits you, etching stops.

Sure Chisel: from Etching 30, each blank has a chance of an extra tile: 10%, rising to 15% at Etching 60 and 20% at 90.

Smelt 1 hexslate with 1 bone ash for 2 blanks (4 XP). Bone ash comes from burning bones at a forge. See Bones and the Guard's Rest.

Warden statues

Spoiler Reveals: a way to make the statues safe

Return a province's seal and its Warden statues are at peace: the guards there stop starting fights, so you can etch safely even at night.

ElementStatue in the game
WatersThe Gullwatch shrine in Saltmere (a small wayside shrine that works like a statue), and the Warden of Waters in Mistmarsh. Monsters near one of the Mistmarsh statues only come out at night, so etch there by day.

Chisels

Hang a chisel on your tool belt to etch faster and sometimes get a bonus tile. Chisels are smithed at a forge.

The etcher's set (apron, cap and gloves) adds more: the gloves give +5% bonus tile chance, and the full set gives +2.5% Etching XP. Bonus tile chance tops out at 20%.

  • Rarer tiles at higher Etching levels.
  • Higher-level Etching perks up to 99.
