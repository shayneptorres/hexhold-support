How to craft

Tap a workbench, a long carpenter's bench that takes up two hexes. There's one in every region's main town; see Where to find the stations. Pick what to make. Most jobs take 4 ticks (2.4 seconds).

Wooden gear

Each kind of log makes staffs, rods and shields. Staffs are for Magic, rods for Fishing. One-handed wands are made from a log and a blank wardglass; see Wands. Bows are under Bows and arrows.

Bows

Carve an unstrung bow at a workbench (a shortbow takes 3 logs, a longbow 4), then string it from your bag, anywhere. Levels and strings for each wood are on the Archery page.

Arrows

  1. Shafts: cut logs at a workbench. Plain logs give 15 shafts, oak 20, willow 30 and ash 50.
  2. Feathers: gather them from hen runs at the farmsteads and gull nests on the coast. Feather 15 shafts at a time to make headless arrows.
  3. Arrowheads: smith them at a forge. See Smithing.
  4. Fletch 15 headless arrows with 15 arrowheads at a workbench.

Pottery

Pottery is a branch of Crafting, and it gives Crafting XP. Prue Crock keeps a potter's yard across the Brin from the watermill in Fairmeadow, and her errand First Firing teaches the whole craft, start to finish.

  1. Dig clay at a clay bank.
  2. Wet and shape it at a potter's wheel. There are wheels in Fairmeadow, Mistmarsh, Highmoor, Cinderreach and Frostpeak.
  3. Fire it in a kiln. Firing can crack a piece; the better your Crafting, the less often it happens.

From Crafting 30 you can glaze your pots too.

Some pots are handy as well as pretty:

  • A pie dish in your bag makes your pies burn less when you bake. You keep the dish.
  • A brew crock in your bag when you brew holds the draught with 2 more sips than a bottle, and comes back empty when it's drunk.

Prue also gives you her book, and its pages show what each clay makes as you go.

Tile satchels

Bigger tile satchels at Crafting 20, 45 and 75.

Drake gear

From Crafting 65 you can work drakehide, and from 72 drake scales riveted with sealsteel rivets, into armour. From 75, wyrmglass makes a staff. See Drake gear.

Sacks, chests and bundles

Crafting also makes the gear for carrying more. It has its own page: Sacks, chests and bundles.

  • Gathering sacks hang on your belt and catch logs, fish or ore when your bag is full.
  • Field chests are set down where you work and hold a lot.
  • Bundles pack several logs, fish or ores into one bag slot.
  • The King's Post carries your load to the bank once you have the post horn.

Frostpine and Heartwood gear is in the recipe list, but those logs can't be chopped yet.
