Saltmere Isle

The windy island where you wake up. A fishing village, a quay, woods, a quarry, and an old fort on the headland.

At a glance

A small island of beach, meadow and woodland. Good for levels 1 to 10.

Places: the village, Eel Quay, Wreckers' Strand, Gullwood, Heron Cove, Tinker's Cut (a quarry), Gull Downs, Crownwake Bay and Gullwatch Point.

What's here

SkillWhere to train
WoodcuttingTrees, oaks and willows
FishingThe sea and a creek. Ask Gaffer Moss what bites.
MiningCopper, tin, iron and hexslate
SmithingThe forge
CookingThe hearth inside the Salted Eel, and the Quay campfire
CraftingThe workbench
EtchingThe Gullwatch shrine, a small wayside Waters shrine set into the old fort wall, where most new players etch their first Waters tiles
BrewingSaltwort, and Hattie's salve still by her cottage

Services: a bank (the gold-roofed tower), a general store, Ida's stall (tiles), the Salted Eel inn (a bed up the stairs for 5 coins), a chapel, and a waystone.

Your home: once you've finished your first days here, the Driftwood Cottage is yours.

People

Hattie (beachcomber), Wenna Gale (harbourmaster), Brannoc (smith), Pell (shipwright), Gaffer Moss (old fisher), Old Tamsin (keeps the Salted Eel), Ida Rook (market stall, rooks, and the island's gossip), Maudie (the bank clerk), and a lamplighter you'll only meet at night.

Danger

  • Drowned oarsmen shamble on the strand. They're weak and won't attack you.
  • The Bone Guard hold the old fort at Gullwatch Point. They keep to themselves by day. The signpost's advice: "Go by day." The keep inside has a door that stays shut to strangers. See Bones and the Guard's Rest.

Mending house: Hattie's spare cot. Your first three falls here are free.

Leaving the island

The ferry from Eel Quay goes to Kingsferry in Fairmeadow, but it needs building first. Pell will tell you what she needs. See Getting around.
