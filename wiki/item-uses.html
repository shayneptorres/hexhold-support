Quick answers

Hold any item in your bag to open its card, which says what it is and what you can do with it right now. This page fills in the rest: where an item gets used across all the skills. Anything you have no use for can be sold at a general store or the market.

Brewing ingredients are listed by name only. Which herb goes with which ingredient is for you to discover; see Brewing.

Bones

ItemUsed for
BonesBurn them at a forge for bone ash (Smithing). Cook fish broth from them with a small raw fish (Cooking). Lay the Bone Guard's to rest (Bones and the Guard's Rest).
Big bonesThe same as bones, but one does the work of two in broth. Also a Brewing ingredient.
Guard bonesBone ash at a forge, and a Brewing ingredient. Some people ask for them too.
Dragon bonesBone ash at a forge.
Bone ashSmelt it with hexslate into blank wardglass (Etching). Make glaze slip for pottery (Crafting 30). A Brewing ingredient, and the base of Warden's ink.

The forges' ore bin holds bones and bone ash too, and the furnace burns bones from it. See Bones and the Guard's Rest for everything bones do.

Ores, bars and smithing

ItemUsed for
Copper, tin, iron, gold and other oresSmelt into bars at a furnace (Smithing). Copper ore is also a Brewing ingredient.
CoalFuel for smelting the harder bars.
Bars (bronze up to starsteel)Smith weapons, armour, tools, arrowheads, studs, nails and rivets at an anvil (Smithing). Some go into Crafting too: a steel bar for the reinforced satchel, bronze for some kit.
Iron studsStudded leather armour (Crafting).
Iron nailsSignboards and other wooden fittings.
Sealsteel rivetsDrake-scale armour (Drake gear).
ArrowheadsTip headless arrows (Archery).
Emberite, rimesilver, starstone, duskiteSmelt into the bars above steel: embersteel, frostsilver, starsteel and deepsteel (Smithing). Where to find them: Where to mine.
HexslateSmelt into blank wardglass for Etching. Also a Brewing ingredient.

Wood

ItemUsed for
Logs of every kindStaffs, wands, fishing rods, shields and bows at a workbench (Crafting). Oak logs also make planks (Crafting 5), and logs go into chests and other kit.
PlanksSignboards, chests and building work.
Bark twineBundles, sacks and lots of kit; one of the most-used things in Crafting. Also a Brewing ingredient.
Arrow shafts, feathersHeadless arrows (Archery).

Hides, fibres and leather

ItemUsed for
CowhideTan it into leather at a workbench (Crafting 1).
LeatherArcher's armour, quivers, satchels and sacks (Crafting).
TallowHardened leather armour and the reinforced satchel.
FlaxFlax bowstrings and canvas quivers.
SinewDry it into a bowstring (Crafting 30), or make a hide quiver.
Drakehide, drake scales, drake sinewDrake gear and the Warden's satchel (Drake gear).
FrostsilkFrostsilk bowstrings.
FleeceNot used in making anything yet. Sell it, or keep some for anyone who asks.

Food and cooking

ItemUsed for
Raw fishCook on a fire or range (Cooking). Small fish also make broth with bones. Some are Brewing ingredients.
Raw meatCook on any fire.
WheatGrind into flour at a mill.
Flour, jug of water, eggs, jug of milk, applesBaking: bread, pies and cakes (Baking). Flour is also a Brewing ingredient.
Bread dough, pie shellBake them in an oven.
JugFill it at a well, a spring or fresh water. A full jug gives four pours.

Clay and pottery

ItemUsed for
Brin, marsh, fire and snow clayWet it at a potter's wheel into soft clay (Pottery).
Soft clayShape it at a wheel into an unfired piece.
Unfired piecesFire them in a kiln.
Glaze slipGlazes a pot before firing.
Pie dish, brew crockCarry a pie dish to burn fewer pies; carry a brew crock to get 2 more sips from a brew.

Magic

ItemUsed for
Element tilesCast spells (Magic). A few also go into special Crafting pieces and Brewing.
Blank wardglassEtch it into tiles at a Warden statue (Etching). It also makes wands.
Element tomesHold in the shield hand for a Magic boost, or rebind into a stronger edition (Tome editions).
Warden's inkRebinding tomes.

Herbs and creature parts

Herbs (bog myrtle, heather, meadowsweet, saltwort, elderflower, firethorn, rimecap, greyleaf and more) and creature parts (eye of newt, ember beetle, golem pebble, gull feather, fish scales, moonmoth wing, troll tooth, wolfman claw) are Brewing ingredients. See Brewing to start working out what they make.

Trophies and keepsakes

Some big monsters drop a trophy, like a huge fang or a scale that never cools. These aren't used in making anything at the moment; they're worth a good price, or keep them as proof. A worn riddle map starts a treasure hunt; see Riddle maps.
