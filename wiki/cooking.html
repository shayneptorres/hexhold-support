Cooking

Cook fish on a fire, and bake bread, pies and cakes in an oven, so you can eat them to heal. Higher levels burn less.

How to cook

Tap a fire with raw fish in your bag. Saltmere has the hearth inside the Salted Eel and the Quay campfire, and the other regions have campfires. The Hearthspark spell (Magic, level 7) lights a short-lived campfire wherever you are. For the best kitchen, use a town Hearthhouse.

Each fish takes 4 ticks (2.4 seconds). Cooked fish show in your bag as "Cooked trout" and so on. Some burn; burnt fish are no good to eat. Your chance of burning falls as your level rises, until you stop burning that fish altogether.

Meat

Raw meat comes from Fairmeadow's sheep and cows, fell rams on Highmoor, wolfmen and Kingsway bandits, among others. Cook it on any fire from Cooking 1 for 30 XP. It burns like shrimp (50% at level 1, never from 34), and cooked meat heals 4.

Fish broth

From Cooking 10 (or the fish's own level, if higher), cook 1 raw small fish (shrimp, sardine, herring, perch or trout) with 2 bones or 1 big bones into fish broth. Better fish are too good for the pot. It gives the fish's cooking XP plus 5, with the same burn chance. See Bones and the Guard's Rest.

Baking

The Cooking skill guide (Skills tab) lists every bake, with All / Fish / Baking tabs. Tap a bake there to open its recipe card, with how many you've baked.

Baking is part of Cooking, and it gives Cooking XP. New to it? Mabry Loaf at the Kingsferry Hearthhouse walks you through your first loaf.

  1. Pick wheat in the Fairmeadow fields. It grows back about 30 seconds after you pick it.
  2. Grind it at a mill. The mill grinds your whole stack of wheat into flour at once. At the Old Mill, carrying wheat gives you a choice of Grind wheat or Trade.
  3. Fill a jug with water. Any well, spring or water butt works, and so does fresh water: rivers, becks, ponds and tarns, but not the sea. There are wells beside the bread ovens and mills. A jug of water holds 4 pours: each dough or pie shell takes one, and you keep the jug.
  4. Mix and bake at a bread oven. You'll find ovens in the farmsteads and at tavern hearths. Once the jug is empty you keep it to fill again. Farmstead and farmhouse ovens only bake, while tavern hearths work as fires too. An oven lists every bake; the ones you can't make yet are greyed out with their level and ingredients.

Once you've added the hearth and range to your The Driftwood Cottage, it's an oven too, and it burns a little less than the others.

The Old Mill shop sells flour, empty jugs, eggs, jugs of milk, apples and raw meat.

Mixing bread dough gives 5 XP, and rolling a pie shell (Cooking 20) gives 10. Carry a clay pie dish (see Pottery) and your pies, tarts and pasties burn less. Pies and cakes are eaten a bite at a time: each bite heals, and what's left goes back in your bag.

Hearthhouses

Every town has a Hearthhouse, a two-hex cook house with a kitchen room inside: Saltmere, Kingsferry, Reedwick, Windle, Thornwick, Emberfall and Kettlepass. Each has its own resident cook to chat with. Ask any of them about baking and they'll send you to Mabry Loaf in Kingsferry, who teaches new bakers in the First Loaf errand.

  • Cooking range: burns food as if your Cooking were 3 levels higher, and each cook takes one tick less than over a fire.
  • Bread oven: each bake takes one tick less than in an outdoor oven.
  • Work table: mix dough and roll pie shells here. It has a water butt too, so you can fill jugs without leaving the room.
  • Pantry chest: holds raw fish and baking things. The range and oven take what they need from the pantry first, then from your bag. There's one pantry shared by every Hearthhouse, so you can fill it in one town and cook in another.

Bank your raw catch in the pantry on the way past, then cook it all at whichever Hearthhouse is nearest.

Extras

About 1 fish in 150 gives a Flame tile for Magic.

Deepgill and heartfin have cooking levels set (up to Heartfin 90) but can't be caught yet. The better the fish, the more it heals and sells for.
