At a glance

Inside the ring of mountains lies the Crown Valley: grey grass, a black moat, and the Hexhold itself. The hardest place in the game so far, for levels 65 to 90.

A road runs right round the moat, and the roads from each gate ramp down to meet it. There's only one way across the water, though: the bridge.

Places: the Lantern camp, the six gates, the moat, the bridge, the Hexhold door, the duel ring and the Captain's Rest.

Getting here: through one of the six Crownwall gates, one on the far side of each province. Each stays shut until you've earned your way through, and how is part of that province's story.

Spoiler Reveals: what opens the gates

Each gate opens for the seal of its own province. See Something for the centre.

Once you've walked through a gate, the Crownwall Gate teleport (Magic 68) can take you back inside it. See Magic.

Services: at the Lantern camp, just inside the South Gate: a bank chest, a campfire and a waystone. There's no shop, forge or workbench in the valley. Ide has bigger plans for the camp, in time (see Late works).

The valley has its own music.

People

Lantern-keeper Ide of the Lanternfolk, who carry the fallen out of places like this. And the Bone Captain, on the bridge.

Danger

Everything here hits hard. See Monsters.

  • Seal colossi stand just inside the gates. They only move if you step on the road they watch.
  • Greyed guards and seal sentinels walk the valley and the foot of the Hexhold walls.
  • The Bone Captain holds the bridge to the Hexhold door, and he isn't friendly. Not at first.

Mending house: Ide's tent at the Lantern camp. Fee 1,200.

The Hexhold door

The door at the end of the bridge has a hollow for each seal. Every seal you hold lights one.

The Bone Captain

Hold enough of the land's trust and the Captain stops fighting and starts talking. That starts a quest, The Bone Captain.

Spoiler Reveals: when the Captain talks to you

He stays hostile until you hold 4 seals.

The Captain's Rest

A roll stone by the bridge, carved with the names of the Crown Watch's officers. It's where the captains' bones are laid to rest. Finding them is up to you. See Bones and the Guard's Rest.

Duels

The Captain spars in the duel ring: a set of bouts, each with its own rules. You need a total level of 750 to take him on, and losing costs you nothing. What the rules are, and what you win, is for you to find out.

The Crown Valley map

Once you own all six province maps, word gets round that Old Hob in Kingsferry has one more map in him. See Maps.
