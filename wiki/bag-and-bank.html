Bag, bank and coins

Your 28-slot bag, the shared bank with 200 slots, and where coins come from and go.

Your bag

  • 28 slots, four across.
  • Most things take a slot each: one log, one fish, one ore. These stack in a single slot: coins, magic tiles, twine, bone ash and planks.
  • Tap an item to use it. Food is eaten if you're hurt, and it's the one you tapped that gets eaten.
  • Hold an item to see its card, with everything you can do with it. The card opens just beside the slot.
  • Hold and drag an item onto another slot to swap them.
  • Sort, beside the bag title, packs everything to the front with coins first and like items together.
  • If the bag looks cramped (big icons, or a wide screen), tap the expand button beside Sort. Your gear and tool belt step aside so the whole bag shows. Tap it again to bring them back.

A full bag stops gathering, unless you've made a sack to catch the overflow. See Sacks, chests and bundles. Anything a quest hands you while your bag is full goes straight to your bank.

The bank

Every bank opens the same storage, so something banked in Saltmere is waiting for you in Kingsferry. It has 200 slots and everything stacks.

Depositing and withdrawing

  • Tap an item to move one; in your bag, it's the slot you tapped that moves. Hold it to choose 1, Half, All or X (type an amount). The choice opens right beside the stack you held.
  • The bank, post boxes and other storage open as one window with your bag beside them. Close it with the single ×.
  • Deposit all empties your bag, your belt sacks and any bundles or loaded chests (they unpack) in one tap.
  • The bank has its own Sort button. One tap groups everything by kind (coins, tools, gear, ores and bars, wood, food, herbs and brews, magic, crafting, drops, hauling, curios, keepsakes), lower tiers first. Tap it again and it sorts A to Z instead.
  • The Pack opened beside a shop or station takes half the screen, like the bank, so both stay in view.

Brews

Brewed draughts are bottles of 4 sips. In your bag each bottle takes a slot; in the bank they stack. See Brewing.

Coins

Coins are an item in your bag. Shops and the cottage take coins from your bag only; town works also take them from your bank.

Ways to earn:

  • selling at a general store, or filling a notice-board order (Shops and markets)
  • your own market stall (Trade and your stall)
  • monster drops and quest rewards
  • riddle-map chests (Riddle maps)
  • selling bones to Ada Pike: 4 coins a bone, 12 a big bone (Bones and the Guard's Rest)

Ways to spend: tiles and gear, the Grand Market, Mags the Pedlar, town works, cottage rooms, and Tamsin's cooking service.

If you owe a mending house after a fall, half of every coin you earn goes toward what you owe. Coins taken out of your bank or grave don't count. See Death and the mending house.
