The short version

Brewing is a skill for getting ready. You forage herbs out in the world, brew them with one more ingredient at a brewing station, and drink the result to play at a higher level for a while.

  • Each brew makes a bottle of 4 sips in your pack. Tap it to drink a sip.
  • Bottles take a pack slot each, and stack in the bank.
  • Pin a brew to the HUD to drink it with one tap.
  • A boost never changes your real level, and never counts for wearing gear, quest requirements or Etching.
  • Brewing counts toward your character level, like every other skill.

Getting started

After Washed Ashore, Hattie on Saltmere has a quest for you, The Mender's Kit. It opens the Brewing skill, teaches you to brew at Hattie's salve still beside her cottage, and sends you off with 5 bottles of Grit Draught. Ask her about it. See Quests.

Everything about Brewing lives on the Brews tab of the Magic menu, next to Spells. The Brews tab lists Your brews, and its brewing book lists every herb, where it grows and the nearest cauldron, and says where made ingredients (like bone ash) are made. Tap ? for a help card. Now and then a one-time tip will suggest a brew that would help, for example against heat, cold or fever, or when you're low on health.

Brewing a draught

  1. Go to a brewing station and tap it.
  2. Pick a herb and a second ingredient from your bag.
  3. If it's a real recipe and your level is high enough, it brews a bottle of 4 sips into your pack (or your bank, if your pack is full), and the draught goes into your Herbal.
  4. If it isn't, it turns into a murky brew. You lose the herb and the ingredient, so guesses aren't quite free. Mother Gloam will buy murky brews for a coin or two.

The Herbal, on the station's sheet, lists every draught. Ones you haven't brewed show as ??? with their level and a hint about the second ingredient.

Bottles and HUD pins

  • Every brew is a bottle of 4 sips (6 from Brewing 45, 8 from 85). Each bottle takes one slot in your pack, and they stack in the bank. Carry a clay brew crock (see Pottery) and the draught goes in it instead, with 2 more sips.
  • Tap a bottle in your pack to drink a sip. Drinking takes your turn, like eating.
  • Pin to HUD: hold a bottle (or food, or another usable) and tap Pin to HUD. You can pin up to 4, and a pinned brew drinks from whichever bottle of it you're carrying.
  • Menders will pour your part-drunk bottles together for free.
  • If you fall, your bottles go into your grave with the rest of your pack.

Drinking

While a boost is running, a badge sits under the place name. Tap it to see how long is left.

  • Skill draughts last a number of actions, so they don't wear off while you walk.
  • Combat and travel draughts last a set time.
  • You can have one draught of each kind going at once. Drinking another of the same kind replaces it.
  • Shape draughts change what you are for a short while. Only one can run at a time, and you can tap its badge to end it early.
  • Stout adds gold hearts on top of your health. Hits take them first, healing can't refill them, and they go when it wears off.

Guise of the Guard: at the Gullwatch keep, the captain asks anyone in a Guise for a watchword. Learning it is a puzzle that opens from Brewing 18, and a wrong answer turns the fort on you.

Giant's Draught: a giant can't go into towns. Villagers shoo you out, and drinking it in town walks you to the edge first.

The Herbal's hints point you to each second ingredient. Some come from other skills, and some you'll only find if you keep your eyes open, sometimes only at a certain time of day. Brewers also have a use for fish scales, which you get at the scaling boards by the water (see Scaling boards).

Some creatures can be picked up, if you're there at the right time: bats at dusk, moonmoths round the Lost Lantern at night, and ember beetles under the Kiln's stones. Some monsters leave useful bits too: wolfmen drop claws, frost trolls drop teeth, and golems drop pebbles.

Warden's ink

From Brewing 20 you can brew Warden's ink at a cauldron, two pots at a time. It isn't a draught: mages use it to rebind their tomes. See Tome editions.

Marsh fever

Some Mistmarsh creatures, bog-drowned and mire golems, can give you marsh fever. A badge shows while you have it. It deals 1 damage every few seconds for about a minute, and catching it again restarts the clock.

  • Marsh Cure ends it at once and keeps it off for a while.
  • Gloam's tonic also ends it. Mother Gloam sells three a day.
  • A fall ends it too.

Brewing 99

Reaching Brewing 99 is a big moment, with four rewards, and something happens at Reedwick's pool.

Spoiler Reveals: the Brewing 99 rewards
  • The Stillwater Mantle, a deep-blue cape whose hem moves like water. It gives off a mist while you wear it, and bubbles on every sip and brew.
  • People greet you as Stillwarden.
  • Menders stop charging you when you fall.
  • A physic garden of your own. Its cauldron adds another 25% chance of a second bottle on every brew, and its fountain tops up your part bottles once a day and gives you 4 fresh bottles.
  • The Six-fold Draught: every skill +6, plus 15%, for 10 minutes. While it runs, no other draught works. You can brew one batch every 24 hours (real time) and drink it once an hour.
