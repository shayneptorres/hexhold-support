How to smith

Tap a forge. There's one in Saltmere, Fairmeadow and Highmoor, Emberfall's great furnace in Cinderreach, and Kettlepass in Frostpeak (Mistmarsh and Greenwood have none). You need a hammer on your tool belt; if you don't have one yet, the smith lends you a wooden mallet the first time.

Pick what to make; the list has a tab for each metal. Most jobs take 4 ticks (2.4 seconds) and never fail.

XP is per bar used: 12.5 for bronze, 25 for iron and 37.5 for steel. A steel platebody gives 187.5 XP.

Embersteel, frostsilver, starsteel and deepsteel follow the same pattern from Smithing 30, 40, 50 and 60 (add 29, 39, 49 or 59 to the bronze level), for 50, 62.5, 75 and 87.5 XP a bar. Each makes its own chisel for Etching, and starsteel and deepsteel make arrowheads too. Where to find their ores: Where to mine.

Sealsteel and hexsteel follow the same pattern from Smithing 70 and 80 (add 69 or 79 to the bronze level), for 100 and 112.5 XP a bar. Each also makes a chisel for Etching. From Smithing 72, one sealsteel bar makes 10 sealsteel rivets (90 XP), used for drake-scale armour. Wyrmfangs make a spear (Smithing 75, with sealsteel), and an axe and a pickaxe (78, with hexsteel). See Drake gear and Tools and the tool belt.

The village forges

Three forges are proper smithies, indoors, each with its own smith: Brannoc's on Saltmere, Aggie Brand's in Kingsferry and Morwen Hollis's in Windle. They work better than an anvil in the open:

What it does
Ore binHolds ore, coal, bones and bone ash by the furnace, and the furnace smelts straight from it. It's one bin shared by all three forges, so ore you leave at one is waiting at the others. Fill it by hand, or have a runner or rook bring your ore.
Smith's strongboxOpens your bank, for ore, coal, bars and smithing tools only.
Carter's crateDeposit only, like a post box: drop your smithing in it and it goes to the bank without the walk.
BellowsEvery smelting and smithing job takes one tick less, for the same XP.
Quench troughNow and then a weapon or armour piece comes out Fine: +1 to its main stat, and it sells for a quarter more (and gives a quarter more Trade XP). The chance is 3% at Smithing 1, rising to about 6% at 99. Tools never come out Fine.

Brannoc has smithing tips if you ask.

Bone ash

At a forge you can also burn bones into bone ash (Smithing 1): bones make 1 ash, big bones 3, Guard bones 2, dragon bones 6. Ash is used in Etching to make extra wardglass. See Bones and the Guard's Rest.

Extras

  • Iron nails (Smithing 10): 1 iron bar makes 10 nails, 12.5 XP. They go into signboards and post houses. See The King's Post.
  • A better hammer can save your bars. See Hammers.
  • About 1 job in 150 turns up a Flame tile.
