Trade and your stall

Rent a stall in Kingsferry's Grand Market, set your prices, and let shoppers buy while you're off doing other things.

Getting a stall

In Kingsferry, two town works from Old Hob:

  1. Reopen three Grand Market stalls (25,000 coins, 30 planks, 30 oak logs).
  2. Rent a Grand Market stall (10,000 coins).

Your stall then stands on the square, east of the bank. Tap it to open it.

Selling

  • Tap Put out on a bag item to set out the whole stack.
  • Each stack starts at 1.3 times its fair price. Fair is what Kingsferry would normally pay, ignoring any flooding.
  • Drag a stack's price slider anywhere from half to 2.5 times fair. A note says how shoppers will take it.
  • Take back returns a stack to your bag.

Shoppers keep coming wherever you are in the world, as long as the game is running. Each picks a stack at random. Sales go into the till; tap Collect to move the coins to your bag.

How shoppers react

Your priceWhat happens
Fair or belowThey always buy
Up to 1.25× fairMost buy
Up to 1.5× fairAbout half buy. This is the sweet spot.
Above 1.5×, up to your greedy capThey haggle, and sometimes pay something between 1.5× and your price
Above your capThey walk off

Your greedy cap starts at 1.8× fair and rises to 2.2× at Trade 99. Higher Trade also wins you more of each haggle.

The Trade skill

Trade levelWhat it gives
14 stacks on your stall, a shopper about every 7 seconds
10A 5th stack
20A 6th stack
30A 7th stack
40An 8th stack
HigherShoppers come more often (twice as often by 99), and you haggle better

You earn 0.1 Trade XP for every coin from stall sales, notice-board orders and Market Warrants.
