Privacy Policy

HexSeal · Last updated October 18, 2026

Home Terms of use

The short version

  • HexSeal collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the game is sent to me or anyone else.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • The game works fully offline. It makes no network requests.
  • Your saves and settings are stored only on your device. An exported save goes only where you send it.

Who is responsible

HexSeal is made by Shayne Torres, an independent developer. Questions about this policy can be sent through the app's TestFlight or App Store page.

What the game stores

To keep your progress, HexSeal stores on your device:

  • Your save: your character's look, skills, items, bank, quests and where you are in the world.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, camera and layout choices.

None of this leaves your device through HexSeal. If you back up your iPhone or iPad with iCloud or a computer, your save may be included in that backup, under Apple's own privacy terms. I can't see it.

If you export a save, HexSeal writes it as a save file in the game's folder of the Files app. It stays there unless you choose to share it, for example by AirDrop, a message or iCloud Drive. Importing a file reads it from your device. HexSeal never uploads saves anywhere.

Deleting the app deletes your saves, including exported files kept in its folder. Settings › Reset save… wipes the save you're playing from inside the game.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. GitHub may log basic request information (such as IP addresses) to run its service; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

HexSeal collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If the game ever adds something that handles your data, such as online features, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
